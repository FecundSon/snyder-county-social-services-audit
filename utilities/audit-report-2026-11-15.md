## Headline findings (only own-site-evidenced, material corrections)

1. **Snyder County Assistance Office — address and phone corrected.** The current office is **83 Maple Lane, Selinsgrove, PA 17870, phone 570-374-8126** (Mon–Fri 8:30 AM–5:00 PM; geocoded to Selinsgrove, Snyder County, 40.80714, -76.88305). PPL's LIHEAP Assistance Offices PDF still lists the old **570 South High Street, Selinsgrove, PA 17870-0056, phone 570-621-3072** — 570-621-3072 is the Schuylkill County number in that same PDF, i.e. a copy error, and 570 South High Street is a real Selinsgrove address but is no longer the County Assistance Office. UGI's county-offices page and Union-Snyder CAA's own energy page both give 83 Maple Lane / 570-374-8126. The LIHEAP direct line **570-372-1721** appears on UGI's county-offices page; a third-party checker reported it disconnected on Oct 26, 2026 — that is third-party only, so it is flagged for live re-check, not asserted here.
2. **PPL CARES / OnTrack — use 1-800-342-5775, not (800) 358-6623.** PPL's own CARES page says "Please call 1-800-342-5775", and its OnTrack and Operation HELP routes give the same number. (800) 358-6623 is a real PPL payment-arrangements line and is what the PA PUC page lists for "PPL (OnTrack)" — that PUC listing is the stale source. Same pattern as found in the Lehigh County utilities audit.
3. **PPL WRAP — intake is 1-888-232-6302, not 1-800-342-5775.** PPL's own WRAP page: free energy assessment, income ≤200% of federal poverty guidelines, age 18+, English/Spanish reps Mon–Fri 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, call **1-888-232-6302**. Union-Snyder CAA's own energy page says "For more information on WRAP, please call (1-800) 342-5775" — that number reaches PPL general customer service, not WRAP intake. (SEDA-COG's own weatherization page independently gives 1-888-232-6302 for PPL WRAP.)
4. **LIHEAP is OPEN for 2026–27 — any listing still showing "LIHEAP closed" is stale.** PA DHS's own page, checked 2026-11-15: "The 2026-2027 LIHEAP season is open! The season runs November 2, 2026 through April 23, 2027." Cash grants $200–$1,000; crisis grants $25 minimum, $1,000 season maximum (FY 2027 Final State Plan); income ≤150% FPIG; apply via COMPASS; hotline 1-866-857-7095 Mon–Fri. A third-party checker showed "LIHEAP is CLOSED" as confirmed Oct 26, 2026 — that was accurate before the Nov 2 opening and is stale now.
5. **LIHWAP is closed — do not list it as available.** The Union-Snyder Human Services Directory (July 2025) lists "LIHWAP Assistance Program as funds are available" via the County Assistance Office. LIHWAP was a temporary federal water-assistance programme; federal funds were exhausted and the programme closed out in 2023–2024 (ACF Office of Community Services: funding no longer available, no new benefits). There is no current LIHWAP application round.
6. **SEDA-COG Weatherization — street spelling and direct number corrected.** PA 211 gives "201 Furnance Road, Lewisburg, PA 17837, (570) 524-4491". The correct spelling is **201 Furnace Road** (geocoded to Lewisburg, Union County — the office is out of county but SEDA-COG is the designated WAP provider for Snyder County per the PA DCED factsheet updated 07/01/2026). Direct weatherization contact is **570-522-7219** (also 1-800-332-6701); (570) 524-4491 ext 7247 is the general SEDA-COG number.
7. **UGI serves only part of Snyder County — UGI programmes are not countywide.** Per UGI's Gas tariff (Pa. P.U.C. No. 7, Supplement No. 62, effective March 29, 2026), UGI gas territory in Snyder County is Selinsgrove and Shamokin Dam boroughs and Middlecreek, Monroe and Penn townships only. PPL Electric serves all 21 municipalities. Listings should not present UGI CAP / Operation Share / LIURP as available to the whole county. (Wikipedia adds Jackson Township; the tariff does not list it.)
