# Snyder County, PA — Housing / Rental Assistance Audit Pilot
Date started: 2026-11-15
County: Snyder County, PA. Category: housing / rental assistance.
Scope: housing and rental assistance only, serving Snyder County residents.
This is the candidate list from public directories; findings are in
audit-report-2026-11-15.md (same folder). Nothing has been sent to PA 211
or any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any outward
report. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Snyder County. Prepared
with AI assistance (Muse).

## Honest limit
PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a
complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on
retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own
sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Snyder
County check is outstanding before any send.

## Rules applied to this list
- No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- No waitlist, funding, or seasonal-window status is presented as
  permanent; any such status is date-stamped to the check date.
- No housing, legal, or financial advice is rewritten; contact and
  listing accuracy only.
- DOMESTIC VIOLENCE: no safe-house address is published, repeated, or
  stored in these files, even if a directory lists one. DV housing is
  excluded from this audit and deferred to the DV category's private
  handling. Transitions of PA (Snyder County office, 570-374-7773) is
  named only in the excluded section, with no address.
- No claim that an organisation is closed/defunct is made from a failed
  page load; such items are UNVERIFIED.
- Do not refer to Keith as a local resident of Snyder County.
- Addresses were checked by exact address, never by ZIP or town name
  alone. Lewisburg, Milton, Mifflinburg, and Lewistown are in other
  counties and are OUT OF SCOPE as locations, even when a programme
  based there serves Snyder residents (those are flagged as
  out-of-county serving routes, not Snyder locations).
