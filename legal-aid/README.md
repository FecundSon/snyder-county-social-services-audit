## Headline corrections

- **MidPenn Legal Services does NOT serve Snyder County.** Its own site lists 18 central-PA counties; Snyder is not among them. The correct regional civil legal-aid provider for Snyder is **North Penn Legal Services, Sunbury office** (133 N. 2nd St., Sunbury — Northumberland County — serving Northumberland, Snyder and Union Counties; intake 877-953-4250, Mon–Thu 9:00–11:30 AM / 1:30–4:00 PM, no Friday intake).
- **Summit Legal Aid does NOT serve Snyder County.** It serves ten western/southwestern PA counties only. Its appearance in a PA 211 Snyder query result is a directory-matching artefact.
- **The Pennsylvania Bar Association Lawyer Referral Service is a PAID referral, not free legal aid.** Initial 30-minute consultation costs no more than $30; current number is **800-932-0311 (select option 2)**, not 800-692-7375 as still shown on North Penn's Snyder resources page. Snyder is among the 47 counties it covers.
- **Pennsylvania SeniorLAW Helpline hours are Mon–Thu 10:00 AM–12:00 PM** (SeniorLAW Center's own current pages), not 10 AM–2 PM as in older secondary sources. Number 1-877-727-7529, for Pennsylvanians 60+, is confirmed.
- **Transitions of PA serves Snyder County but its main office is in Lewisburg (Union County), not Snyder County.** 24-hour hotline 1-800-850-7948 confirmed; do not present the Lewisburg address as a Snyder location.
- **North Penn intake hours are not office hours, and there is no Friday intake.** Online intake is available anytime.
