# Snyder County, PA — Housing / Rental Assistance Audit Report
Date checked: 2026-11-15
County: Snyder County, PA. Category: housing / rental assistance.
Method: pilot list built from public directories (retrievable PA 211
listings, Union-Snyder CAA directory, Inglis Quick Start guide, PA DHS
documents, secondary directories, housing-authority and nonprofit
listings); every item then checked against the organisation's own
website/page, via public web search and page-text fetches. Own site
beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. No
organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted. The auditor is NOT
a local resident of Snyder County. Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).

to any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any outward
report. This audit does not duplicate the shelters audit's shelter-only

## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site evidence, material to someone trying
to use the service. All other items are in the per-listing table below.

1. **Union-Snyder CAA ERAP listing is stale — remove ERAP from live
   help.** PA DHS's ERAP agency-contacts PDF (October 1, 2022) names
   Union-Snyder Community Action Agency, 713 Bridge Street, Suite 10,
   Selinsgrove, PA 17870, 570-374-0181, ERAP@union-snydercaa.org, as
   the Snyder County ERAP contact. PA DHS states ERAP1 ended
   September 30, 2022 and ERAP2 ended September 30, 2025. The CAA's
   own current Housing Assistance page (union-snydercaa.org/
   housing-assistance, checked 2026-11-15) lists Rapid Rehousing and
   HAP only — no ERAP. Any live ERAP listing for Snyder County is
   stale and must not be repeated as current help.
2. **Snyder County Assistance Office — current address is 83 Maple
   Lane, Selinsgrove, PA 17870, 570-374-8126.** Retire 570 South High
   Street, Selinsgrove, as the current address; it appears only in
   older directories (PPL LIHEAP office list, ebtcard.net,
   financialhelpresources.com). The 83 Maple Lane address is the
   address on pa.gov's county assistance office information and on
   UGI's county-office list, with toll-free 1-866-713-8584 and LIHEAP
   570-372-1721. ESA is a DHS/CAO route via COMPASS; the CAO is a
   benefits office, not a walk-in housing service.
3. **USDA Rural Housing — ZIP corrected, and a wrong phone retired.**
   USDA's own Pennsylvania contacts page (rd.usda.gov, updated
   2026-07-27) gives 359 East Park Drive, Suite 4, Harrisburg,
   PA 17110-2747, main telephone (717) 237-2299. The Inglis Snyder
   County guide gives 17111 and 717-237-2186; retire both — 17111 is
   used on other USDA pages for the same building, but the contacts
   page for the State Office itself gives 17110-2747, and 237-2186
   is a separate USDA single-family-housing document-delivery line,
   not the State Office main number. Regional route only; rural-
   eligibility boundaries apply.
4. **Middlecreek Village Apartments — adopt the manager's address:
   3340 Freeburg Road, Middleburg, PA 17842, 570-374-7224.**
   Monarch Management Group's own property page gives 3340 Freeburg
   Road, Box 13, Middleburg, PA 17842, 1- and 2-bedroom units,
   "Accepting Applications". Retire the "Freeburg Rd, Kreamer,
   PA 17833" form (affordablehousingonline.com) and the "3140
   Freeburg Rd" variant (hudhouses.us) as current addresses. USDA
   Section 515 rural rental housing; income-restricted, income-based
   rent.
5. **PAHAF is closed — retire the "as funds are available" listing.**
   PAHAF's own site (pahaf.org) states the programme is no longer
   accepting new applications, and that it will not pay or approve
   forward mortgage assistance for any months past June 2025. The
   Union-Snyder CAA Human Services Directory (last updated July
   2025) still lists "PAHAF Assistance Fund, as funds are
   available, (888) 987-2423". That directory entry is stale; do not
   send anyone to PAHAF as live help. HEMAP (item 17) is the live
   foreclosure-prevention route.
6. **Snyder County Housing Authority — own-site address is 106 Drake
   Court, Middleburg, PA 17842, 570-837-3979 — not 103 Drake Court.**
   The Inglis Snyder County guide gives "103 Drake Court". The
   Authority's own homepage, its Shade View page, and its May 2026
   Owner-Tenant Guide all give 106 Drake Court, Middleburg,
   PA 17842, hours Monday–Friday 9am–4pm. Retire 103 Drake Court.
   Separately, the Section 8 HCV waitlist status is UNRESOLVED:
   affordablehousingonline.com says the HCV list is closed (last
   opened November 9, 2026 and closed the same day); a November 15,
   2026 aggregator page says Public Housing open, Section 8 closed;
   a CornerHelp page confirmed October 20, 2026 says both waitlists
   open; the Authority's own 2022 flyer says applications are being
   accepted. Publish NEITHER version as fact until the Authority's
   own site or office confirms it.
7. **Snyder County HAP is ONE county fund, not a second source of
   help.** DHS Appendix E (updated February 24, 2026) names
   Union-Snyder Community Action Agency as the Snyder County HAP
   agency (570-374-0181 ext. 130), and the county human-services
   plan states CAA administers HAP (and HSDF) for Snyder County.
   Items 3 and 5 in this audit are the same fund under two labels:
   HAP intake is through CAA's Housing Assistance programme. Do not
   double-count HAP as a separate source of rent help. HAP
   eligibility per CAA's own page: income sufficient to cover rent,
   utilities, and basic living expenses; income cannot exceed 200%
   of poverty; those under 80% of poverty are forwarded to the
   County Assistance Office for Emergency Shelter Allowance
   determination.
