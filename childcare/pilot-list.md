## How this list was built

PA 211 taxonomy searches retrievable by text fetch returned mainly Snyder County Children and Youth Services items (all at 713 Bridge Street, Selinsgrove) and Domestic Relations. No complete PA 211 county-filtered child-care list could be pulled. The pilot was therefore built from: (a) retrievable PA 211 items, (b) the Union-Snyder CAA Human Services directory (last updated July 2025, union-snydercaa.org), (c) the LIU 18 Snyder County Resources sheet, (d) SUMMIT Early Learning's own locations/contact pages, (e) PA government pages (pa.gov ELRC list, CMSU, Snyder County government), and (f) secondary child-care listings (childcarecenter.us, Winnie, Care.com, localoffices.org, Susquehanna Kids preschool listing). Claim sources are listed per row. Nothing below has yet been verified against an own site at this stage — verification is in `audit-report-2026-11-15.md`.

## Notes on the pilot as found

- **No waitlist status was stated on any own site checked** for this pilot; do not publish waitlist claims.
- **No Freeburg, McClure, Port Trevorton, Richfield, Mount Pleasant Mills, Kratzerville, Paxtonville, Beavertown, Shamokin Dam or Hummels Wharf child-care centre was found** in the retrievable sources — childcarecenter.us explicitly says "no child development centers" in Freeburg and in Mount Pleasant Mills. That is absence from these sources, not proof none exists.
- **School-year vs summer:** SUMMIT Pre-K Counts and CSD programmes are school-year / school-day programmes; SUMMIT's own newsletter notes school-age programmes run in summer at Selinsgrove Elementary & Intermediate — hours for summer were not verified per site.
- **Conflicting secondary claims carried into the pilot (to be resolved in the audit report):** SUMMIT Susquehanna 550 vs 590 University Ave; Loving Hearts phone (570) 374-8954 vs (570) 374-4895 and a possible second Middleburg site; Heaven's Little Angels capacity 65 vs 75 and subsidised-care participation; Selinsgrove Elementary 600 N Broad St vs 600 Broad St.
- **A conflicting ELRC claim was found and excluded from the pilot as a separate row:** Community Connections for Children (cccforpa.org) describes itself as ELRC for Regions 9 & 10 and lists Snyder among counties served. pa.gov and elrc8.org both place Snyder in **ELRC Region 8**. The pa.gov/elrc8.org position is used; the CCC claim is flagged in the audit report for a pre-send check, not treated as a second ELRC for Snyder.
- No children's personal information or photos were collected or published.
