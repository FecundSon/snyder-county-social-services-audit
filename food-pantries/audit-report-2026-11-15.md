# Snyder County, PA — Food Pantry Listing Audit Report

**County:** Snyder County, PA (county seat Middleburg; largest town Selinsgrove; other towns Shamokin Dam, Freeburg, Port Trevorton, McClure, Beaver Springs, Richfield, Mount Pleasant Mills, Kratzerville, Paxtonville, Beavertown, Hummels Wharf)
**Category:** Food pantries / food distributions
**Date checked:** 2026-11-15
**Method:** Public web search and page-text fetch only. Each listing was checked against the organisation's own website / own page (including its own Facebook page). Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. Every address was verified by exact geocoding (map.geocode, full street address), never by ZIP or town name alone. No organisation, pantry, or directory was contacted.
**Status: NOT SENT YET.** Nothing in this report has been sent to statewide PA 211, the Central Pennsylvania Food Bank, Union-Snyder Community Action Agency, or any pantry. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Snyder County.
**Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).**

## Honest limit

PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Snyder County check is outstanding before any send.

## Headline findings

Only the corrections strong enough to send — own-site evidence, and material to someone trying to use the service:

1. **Loaves and Fishes, St. Paul's UCC, 400 N Market St, Selinsgrove — the distribution day is the last FRIDAY of the month, 12:30–2:00pm, not the 4th Thursday.** The Central Pennsylvania Food Bank map as republished by HelpByState gives "4th Thursday, 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM and 3:00 PM to 5:30 PM". Against that: CAA's own 2020 and 2021 Snyder County schedules ("Last Friday of each month, 12:30 PM – 2:00 PM"), the Union-Snyder Agency on Aging 2025 guide ("last Friday of month 12:30-2:00 pm"), and St. Paul's own site / Susquehanna University volunteer listings for Friday, October 30, 2026 and Friday, November 27, 2026, 9:00am–5:00pm at 400 N Market St ("volunteers will pack and distribute food throughout the day"). The 3:00–5:30pm second session in the CPFB-map version has no support in any CAA, church, or guide source found. For Snyder County residents east of Rt. 104; income-based; households register for one pantry only.
2. **Grace Works / Grace Food Pantry, Grace Covenant Community Church, Middleburg — Monday hours are now 9:00–11:00am, not 10:00–11:00am.** The CPFB map as republished by HelpByState and the 2025 Agency on Aging guide both give Monday 10–11am and Wednesday 5–6pm, and the church's own Facebook page confirmed 10–11am / 5–6pm in February 2026. The church's own Grace Food Pantry flyer posted November 9, 2026 says "We are open Monday 9-11AM", with November 23 open 9–11am and 3:30–5:30pm and closed November 26 for Thanksgiving. Wednesday 5–6pm is unchanged in the evidence found. Open to Snyder County residents; proof of residency required (per the church's own Facebook replies).
3. **Five Barley Loaves is in McClure, not Beaver Springs — and its hours are 10:00–11:00am on the last Friday, not 10:00am–12:00pm or 10:00–11:30am.** A secondary aggregator places it "in Beaver Springs, Pennsylvania 17812". CAA's schedules, the Agency on Aging guide, and Trinity Lutheran Church McClure's own site all place it at 6 W Specht St, McClure (white parish building, corner of Specht & Zeller), last Friday of the month. Trinity's own site (checked 2026-11-15) gives 10–11am, with November and December moved to the Friday before Thanksgiving and Christmas. The 10am–12pm figure is from CAA's 2020/2021 schedules; the 10:00–11:30am figure is the CPFB map as republished. For Snyder County residents west of Rt. 104.
4. **Martha's Table, All Saints Episcopal Church, 129 N Market St, Selinsgrove — a free community MEAL, not a grocery pantry, and the phone is (570) 374-8289, not (570) 374-9872.** The church's own Martha's Table page: free takeout meal, 1st, 3rd and 5th Wednesdays (except December), doors open 4:30pm, dinner served until 5:30pm or the food is all gone; St. Pius X cooks and serves on all 5th Wednesdays; parish office (570) 374-8289. PA 211's retrievable Free Meals listing agrees on address and (570) 374-8289. The (570) 374-9872 number and a 5:00–6:00pm time appear only in secondary republishing (HelpByState / Agency on Aging guide).
5. **Union-Snyder CAA appears twice on PA 211 for one service: "Emergency Food Program" and "Emergency Food Clearinghouse" are the same office, address, phone, and hours** — 713 Bridge St, Suite 10, Selinsgrove, (570) 374-0181, Mon/Wed/Thu/Fri 8:30am–4:00pm, Tue 9:30am–4:00pm. List it once. It is emergency food between scheduled pantries (call for screening; pick-up at the office; delivery case by case), not a scheduled weekly pantry.
6. **Brookside Ministries Church, 11 Stetler Ave, Shamokin Dam — the published "Sunday–Thursday, 9:00am–12:00pm" pantry hours could not be verified as pantry hours.** The CPFB map as republished gives those hours, but no pantry schedule appears on the church's own site, and the hours resemble church/office hours (directory listings give Sun 9:30am–1:00pm, Mon–Thu 7:30am–1:30pm). Address (570) 743-5683 confirmed; pantry schedule UNVERIFIED — do not publish the 9am–12pm slot as pantry hours without a live check.
7. **MilitaryShare, Post 25, 524 S Market St, Selinsgrove — for military households only, pre-registration required; not a general walk-in pantry.** Central Pennsylvania Food Bank's own MilitaryShare page lists Snyder County — Post 25 Auxiliary Selinsgrove, 570.541.5155, "Clients must call ahead to pre-register and for distribution schedule." ## What this report does NOT claim

- It does not claim to be a complete directory of Snyder County food pantries. PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites. A live-browser PA 211 Snyder County check is outstanding before any send, and it may surface pantries not in this pilot (in particular any PA 211 Food Pantries taxonomy entries that text fetch could not retrieve).
- It does not claim any pantry is closed or defunct. Brookside Ministries Church (#5) and Victory Baptist Church (#16) are UNVERIFIED — that is a gap in public web evidence, not a finding — and must not be published as fact.
- It does not claim hours are current beyond 2026-11-15. Pantry hours change for holidays: Grace Works closed November 26, 2026 for Thanksgiving; Five Barley Loaves moves to the Friday before Thanksgiving and Christmas in November and December; Martha's Table does not run in December; Bread of Life and Christ's Prayer Pantry shift their Saturday dates around holidays. Seasonal and holiday status is date-stamped in the entries above.
- It does not claim the phone numbers flagged as conflicting are settled: Loaves & Fishes (570) 374-8749 (church office, own site) vs (570) 374-1458 (Agency on Aging guide only); Love in Action (570) 568-6835 vs (570) 713-4145 (out of scope, for the Union audit).
- It does not rewrite expert advisory content. Only contact information, structure, and metadata (names, addresses, phones, hours, eligibility, meal-vs-pantry and monthly-vs-weekly distinctions, duplicates, county) were audited.
- A live website does not by itself prove a pantry is currently operating. Hours from a church/office page are not pantry hours, and are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible (notably Brookside Ministries).
- Monthly distributions are not weekly pantries, and meals are not grocery pantries; both distinctions are flagged wherever they applied (Loaves & Fishes, Five Barley Loaves, Helping Hands, Bread of Life, Christ's Prayer Pantry, Martha's Table, Shepherd's Pie, Meals on Wheels, MilitaryShare).
- The auditor is NOT a local resident of Snyder County. Corrections, challenges, and updates from statewide PA 211, the Central Pennsylvania Food Bank, Union-Snyder CAA, or the pantries themselves are welcome — that is the point of staging this here first.
