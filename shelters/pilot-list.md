# Snyder County, PA — Shelters Pilot List

**County:** Snyder County, Pennsylvania (county seat Middleburg; largest
town Selinsgrove; other towns: Shamokin Dam, Freeburg, Port Trevorton,
McClure, Beaver Springs, Richfield, Mount Pleasant Mills, Kratzerville,
Paxtonville, Beavertown, Hummels Wharf). Rural county in central
Pennsylvania. Neighbouring counties: Union, Northumberland, Juniata,
Mifflin, Centre. **ZIP code does NOT settle county — exact-address
geocoding does.** Nearby towns such as Lewisburg, Milton, Mifflinburg and
Lewistown are in other counties and are OUT OF SCOPE.

**Category:** Shelters (emergency, family, youth, veterans).
**Directory:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Date compiled:** 2026-11-15. **Status: not sent yet. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to PA 211 or any organisation. No organisation was contacted.**

**Auditor:** Keith Conklin. Keith is NOT a local resident of Snyder County.
This audit was prepared with assistance from his AI assistant, Muse.

## How this list was built

PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-15
did not return a complete extractable Snyder County-filtered result list.
This pilot is therefore a **partial pilot**, anchored on:

- PA 211 search results retrievable by text fetch (Overnight Shelters,
  Permanent Housing, Emergency Shelter Clearinghouses, Domestic Violence
  Shelters searches, which surface Snyder County items among statewide
  results, and individual PA 211 records for Union-Snyder CAA and
  Transitions of PA);
- the Inglis / PHFA Snyder County Quick Start Housing and Services
  Information guide (Feb 2025);
- the Union-Snyder Community Action Agency's own Union & Snyder County
  Human Services Directory (July 2025 edition, and the 2018 edition for
  older listings);
- the Union & Snyder County Reentry Coalitions resource guide, 2nd edition,
  June 2025;
- the Eastern Pennsylvania Continuum of Care (Connect To Home) Coordinated
  Entry access-point lists (updated 14 Sep 2026 and 27 Oct 2026);
- the PA Department of Human Services Homeless Assistance Program contact
  list (updated 24 Feb 2026);
- shelterlistings.org / homelessshelterdirectory.org county and city pages
  (secondary — never treated as authoritative);
- organisations' own websites, as returned by search on 2026-11-15.

Each item below records the directory claim as found, and the question that
was open before checking. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org filtered
to Snyder County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.

**Honest limit:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch
cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot
anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus
organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser
PA 211 Snyder County check is outstanding before any send.

**Safety note:** One item in this pilot (Transitions of PA, item 6) is a
domestic-violence emergency shelter in a confidential location. It is listed
by helpline/intake phone only; no shelter address is published anywhere in
these files. A second item (By Grace Women's Home, item 7) is a women's
transitional home whose street address is not published by the organisation
in any source found; it is listed by town and phone only, and no street
address should be added.

## Gaps noted while building the list (not listings)

- **No general emergency shelter building was found inside Snyder County.**
  The county's in-county provision is intake, assessment, rehousing and
  prevention (Union-Snyder CAA, Coordinated Entry), one women's transitional
  home (By Grace, Middleburg — opening/status unverified), and a
  domestic-violence emergency shelter reached only by hotline (Transitions
  of PA). A Snyder County resident needing an emergency shelter bed is, on
  the evidence found, referred out of county — principally to Haven Ministry
  in Sunbury (Northumberland County), the closest general emergency shelter
  serving Snyder residents.
- **No dedicated youth emergency shelter was found** serving Snyder County.
  No unaccompanied-youth shelter for the county appears in PA 211, the
  Union-Snyder directory, or the Inglis guide. The nearest youth shelter
  found, NEPA Youth Shelter, serves north-eastern Pennsylvania counties, not
  Snyder. This is a gap in provision, not a listing error.
- **No dedicated winter / warming (Code Blue) overnight shelter was found
  for Snyder County** in this check. The only warming provision found
  in-county is daytime warming/cooling at Snyder County Libraries
  (Selinsgrove). Code Blue results returned for "Snyder" were for other
  counties (Central Bucks, Berwick, Luzerne). A January 2026 local news
  report said Haven Ministry in Sunbury was already full with a waiting
  list of about 60 people during extreme cold — date-stamped 23 Jan 2026,
  not a current availability claim.
- **No Salvation Army Corps exists in Snyder County.** The nearest Corps are
  Sunbury and Milton (both Northumberland County). The "Sunbury/Selinsgrove"
  label in directories means the Sunbury Corps serves the Selinsgrove area;
  it does not mean there is a Selinsgrove Corps. A volunteer Service
  Extension committee covers Lewisburg/Milton (Union/Northumberland).
- **No separate family shelter** beyond Haven Ministry (families, out of
  county) and Transitions of PA (survivors with children, confidential
  location) was found for Snyder County residents.
