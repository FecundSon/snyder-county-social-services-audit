# Snyder County, PA — Shelter Listing Audit

**County:** Snyder County, Pennsylvania (county seat Middleburg; largest
town Selinsgrove; other towns: Shamokin Dam, Freeburg, Port Trevorton,
McClure, Beaver Springs, Richfield, Mount Pleasant Mills, Kratzerville,
Paxtonville, Beavertown, Hummels Wharf). Rural county in central
Pennsylvania.
**Category:** Shelters (emergency, family, youth, veterans).
**Directory checked against:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Date checked:** 2026-11-15.
**Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide
PA 211, to any shelter, or to any other directory. No organisation was
contacted.**

**Auditor:** Keith Conklin. Keith is NOT a local resident of Snyder County.
This audit was prepared with assistance from his AI assistant, Muse.

## Method and limits — please read before using this

- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-15. No shelter, no
  directory, and no one else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over third-party
  directories. Where an organisation's own site did not state a fact, that
  item is marked CLARIFIED or UNVERIFIED in the table — a gap in public web
  evidence, not a finding, and it must not be published as fact.
- **Direct page-text fetch was rate-limited on 2026-11-15:** repeated
  `browser.open` calls returned an upstream 429 ("exceeded the maximum
  number of requests allowed within a given time frame") and were not
  retried. Own-site claims below therefore rest on the organisations' own
  pages as returned by search on 2026-11-15, plus PA 211 records and the
  Inglis/PHFA Snyder County guide — not on a full page fetch of each own
  site. A live-browser re-check of the own sites (and of PA 211) is
  outstanding before any send. Nothing in this audit should be sent until
  that re-check is done.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed or
  missing page is reported as unverified, never as a closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a shelter is currently operating.
- Office hours from a church/office/centre page are not shelter hours, and
  are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- **Bed availability is never published as a static fact in this audit.**
  Only the intake phone and intake process are reported. A January 2026
  news report that Haven Ministry was full with a waiting list is recorded
  only as date-stamped context, not as a current availability claim.
- **One shelter in this audit is a domestic-violence shelter in a
  confidential location (item 6, Transitions of PA). Its shelter address is
  NOT published anywhere in these files.** It is counted (1 confidential-
  location item) and its public 24-hour hotline and public Snyder County
  office phone are given, because the organisation itself publishes those.
  PA 211 lists this record with a Lewisburg (Union County) address; that
  address is NOT repeated in these files and must never be treated as the
  shelter location. The shelter location must never be added to a public
  copy of this audit.
- **A second item (item 7, By Grace Women's Home, Middleburg) is a women's
  transitional home whose street address is not published by the
  organisation in any source found.** It is listed by town and phone only,
  and no street address should be added to it either.
- **Limit, stated plainly:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven,
  and a text fetch on 2026-11-15 did not return a complete extractable
  Snyder County-filtered result list. This is therefore a **partial pilot**,
  anchored on PA 211 items retrievable by text fetch plus the Inglis/PHFA
  Snyder County guide, the Union-Snyder CAA Human Services Directory
  (July 2025), the Eastern PA Continuum of Care access-point lists, the PA
  DHS Homeless Assistance Program list, and organisations' own sites. It is
  NOT a complete county directory, and a live-browser check of
  search.pa211.org filtered to Snyder County is still outstanding before any
  send to PA 211.
- **Honest limit:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch
  cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot
  anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus
  organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser
  PA 211 Snyder County check is outstanding before any send.
- Snyder County is a small rural county. The in-county shelter stock is
  genuinely tiny: **no general emergency shelter building was found inside
  Snyder County at all.** What exists in-county is intake, assessment,
  rehousing and prevention (Union-Snyder CAA / Coordinated Entry), one
  women's transitional home (By Grace, Middleburg — opening/status
  unverified), and a domestic-violence emergency shelter reached only by
  hotline (Transitions of PA). A Snyder County resident needing an
  emergency shelter bed is, on the evidence found, referred out of county —
  principally to Haven Ministry in Sunbury (Northumberland County). The
  pilot therefore includes the county's housing-adjacent programmes and the
  near neighbours most likely to be confused with Snyder County listings,
  so the exclusions are on the record too.
- ZIP code does not settle county. Every in-county item below was checked
  against its exact address by geocoding, not its ZIP code. Geocoding
  confirmed: 713 Bridge St, Selinsgrove — Snyder County; 83 Maple Ln,
  Selinsgrove — Snyder County; 106 Drake Ct, Middleburg — Snyder County;
  1043 S Front St, Sunbury — Northumberland County; 21 Gate House Dr,
  Danville — Montour County; 13 Depot St, Lewistown — Mifflin County;
  217 E Nittany Ave, State College — Centre County; 40 S 4th St, Sunbury
  and 30 Center St, Milton — Northumberland County; 8 N Grove St,
  Lock Haven — Clinton County.

## Headline findings

1. **Snyder County has no general emergency shelter building of its own.**
   The only PA 211 "Overnight Shelters" items that resolve to Snyder County
   are Coordinated Entry / assessment records at the Union-Snyder CAA
   office (713 Bridge St, Suite 10, Selinsgrove), not shelter beds. The
   CAA's own Housing Assistance page describes Rapid Rehousing (described
   by the CAA itself as "a transitional housing program"), the Homeless
   Assistance Program, and referral to the County Assistance Office for
   Emergency Shelter Allowance — intake and rehousing help, not an
   on-site shelter. Any directory that presents 713 Bridge Street as an
   emergency shelter address should say instead: Coordinated Entry access
   site and housing-assistance intake, Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:00pm, walk-ins
   accepted, phone intake accepted, (570) 374-0181, or call 2-1-1. Do not
   send a person in crisis to that office expecting a bed for the night.
2. **The Snyder County Assistance Office address in older listings is
   wrong.** Current authoritative sources (PA DHS county assistance office
   information via pa.gov; UGI's county-office list; SNAP-office listings)
   all give **83 Maple Lane, Selinsgrove, PA 17870, (570) 374-8126**,
   toll-free 1-866-713-8584. Older aggregators still show the office at
   570 S High St, Selinsgrove. Directories still showing 570 S High St
   should be corrected to 83 Maple Lane. (A 2023 Union-Snyder directory
   also printed the Snyder and Union County Assistance Office emergency
   numbers swapped; the July 2025 edition has them right — Snyder
   (570) 374-8126, Union (570) 524-2201.)
3. **Transitions of PA — confirmed by hotline only, and its location must
   stay confidential.** The domestic-violence emergency shelter serving
   Snyder County is confirmed on the organisation's own site and on
   WomensLaw.org (updated 15 Jul 2026): 24-hour hotline **1-800-850-7948**,
   Snyder County office (570) 374-7773, services free, voluntary and
   confidential, emergency shelter for all genders, serving Union, Snyder,
   Northumberland and now Clinton Counties. PA 211 lists this record with
   a Lewisburg (Union County) address; that address is NOT repeated here
   and must not be treated as the shelter location — it is an office
   address at most, and the shelter itself is in a confidential location.
   PA 211 should list this record by hotline only, or clearly as an office
   / intake point, never as a shelter address.
4. **"Susquehanna Valley Women in Transition" is not a second shelter — it
   is Transitions of PA's former name.** Older Central Susquehanna Valley
   resource lists carry "Susquehanna Valley Women in Transition" (SWIT)
   with the same Snyder County phone (570) 374-7773 and the same toll-free
   hotline 1-800-850-7948 as Transitions of PA. Directories carrying both
   names are double-counting one organisation. SWIT should be removed or
   merged into the Transitions of PA record.
5. **For Snyder County veterans, the SSVF provider is the Veterans
   Multi-Service Center — not the Veterans Leadership Program.** VMC's own
   SSVF page lists Snyder County in its Central Pennsylvania service area
   (Lock Haven office, 8 N Grove St, Suite 2, Lock Haven, PA 17745,
   844-226-0368, Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:30pm; also a Coordinated Entry access
   site for veterans). VLP's own SSVF county list and the VA FY2026 SSVF
   provider list both omit Snyder County (VLP serves western-PA counties
   including Centre and Warren, but not Snyder). Any Snyder County listing
   of VLP SSVF is wrong; the Snyder SSVF intake is VMC on 844-226-0368.
   No PA 211 record claiming VLP serves Snyder was found in this check, so
   this is a guardrail for future listings, not a found PA 211 error.
6. **By Grace Women's Home (Middleburg) cannot be confirmed as open, and
   its phone number conflicts.** The 2018 Union-Snyder directory gives
   (570) 259-6132; a Central PA CareerLink resource list gives
   (570) 765-7301. The organisation's own Facebook posts (Sep 2026) say it
   was preparing to open its doors in October 2026, and a Jan 2024 Daily
   Item profile describes a 12-bed home for women after incarceration,
   homelessness or substance use — but no own website was found, and no
   street address is published anywhere found. Do not publish either phone
   number as verified, and do not add a street address. Re-check with the
   organisation before any send.
7. **The closest general emergency shelter serving Snyder residents is
   Haven Ministry, Sunbury — in Northumberland County, not Snyder County.**
   Haven Ministry (1043 S Front St, Sunbury, PA 17801, (570) 286-1672,
   geocoded Northumberland County) describes itself as providing emergency
   shelter, food, referral help and life-skills education for Snyder, Union
   and Northumberland Counties, since 1991. Secondary listings add that
   referral can be self-referral, a background check is required, and
   6-months' area residency is required — those details rest on a secondary
   aggregator, not Haven's own site, and must be re-checked before they are
   sent anywhere. A 23 Jan 2026 news report said Haven was full with a
   waiting list of about 60 people during extreme cold; that is
   date-stamped context, not current availability.
8. **No youth shelter and no winter/warming overnight shelter serve Snyder
   County.** No dedicated emergency shelter for unaccompanied youth was
   found for Snyder County; the nearest youth shelter found (NEPA Youth
   Shelter) serves north-eastern Pennsylvania counties, not Snyder. No
   Code Blue / winter overnight shelter was found for Snyder County; the
   only in-county warming provision found is daytime warming/cooling at
   Snyder County Libraries, 1 N High St, Selinsgrove. These are gaps in
   provision, not listing errors, and they are recorded here so they are not
   mistaken for oversights.
9. **There is no Salvation Army shelter in Snyder County.** The nearest
   Corps are Sunbury (40 S 4th St, Sunbury, (570) 286-4131) and Milton
   (30 Center St, Milton, (570) 742-4231), both in Northumberland County,
   and neither has an on-site shelter confirmed on the Salvation Army's own
   site. The "Sunbury/Selinsgrove" label in directories means the Sunbury
   Corps serves the Selinsgrove area, not that there is a Selinsgrove
   Corps. Lewisburg is covered only by a volunteer Service Extension
   committee, (570) 492-2865.

CORRECTED = directory claim is wrong on own-site / authoritative evidence.

an emergency shelter, duplicate address, specialised population, or a
detail that rests on a secondary source). UNVERIFIED = could not be
confirmed from the organisation's own site. OUT OF SCOPE = not Snyder
County, PA, or not a shelter listing for this county.

## What this report does NOT claim

- It does **not** claim to be a complete directory of Snyder County shelters.
  PA 211's Snyder County-filtered list could not be pulled in full by text
  fetch, and a live-browser check is still outstanding. Items may exist in
  PA 211 that are not in this pilot.
- It does **not** claim any organisation is closed or defunct. Nothing in
  this audit is a closure finding — including Shelter Services, Inc. in
  Lewistown (a 2026 report said it may be forced to close if resources are
  not found) and By Grace Women's Home (whose October 2026 opening was
  announced but not confirmed as completed).
- It does **not** publish bed availability, waitlist position, or current
  occupancy for any shelter. Only intake phones and intake processes are
  reported. The January 2026 report that Haven Ministry was full with a
  waiting list of about 60 people is date-stamped context from 23 Jan 2026,
  not a current claim.
- It does **not** publish the location of the Transitions of PA shelter, or
  of any other confidential domestic-violence safe house. Two items in this
  audit (items 6 and 7) are counted only / listed by town and phone only.
- It does **not** claim the Union-Snyder CAA runs a shelter bed: its own
  site describes rapid rehousing, homeless assistance and referrals, not an
  on-site shelter.
- It does **not** claim Haven Ministry's referral conditions (self-referral,
  background check, 6-months' area residency) from Haven's own site — those
  details come from a secondary aggregator (shelterlistings.org) and must be
  re-checked with Haven before they are sent anywhere.
- It does **not** claim the Coordinated Entry call-centre hours from a
  single source: PA 211 gives Central Valley 211 as Mon–Fri 9am–4pm, while
  the CoC's own older access-site list gives call-centre hours of
  9:00am–7:45pm. Re-check before sending.
- It does **not** claim either By Grace phone number is current, and it does
  not claim a Family Promise programme serves Snyder County — both are
  UNVERIFIED.
- It does **not** claim the VLP SSVF correction is a found PA 211 error: no
  PA 211 record claiming VLP serves Snyder was found in this check. It is a
  guardrail, evidenced by VLP's own county list omitting Snyder.
- It does **not** claim a youth shelter or a winter/warming overnight shelter
  exists for Snyder County — none was found, and that absence is a gap in
  provision, not proof that no informal arrangement exists.
- Direct page-text fetch was rate-limited (upstream 429) on 2026-11-15, so
  own-site claims in this audit rest on the organisations' own pages as
  returned by search, not on full page fetches. A live-browser re-check of
