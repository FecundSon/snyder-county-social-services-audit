# Snyder County, PA — Social Services Listing Audits

**All categories checked: 2026-11-15. Status: staged for publication in
Keith's own repo first. Nothing in this audit has been sent to PA 211
or any organisation as a direct message.**

## What this is
One county, twelve public categories — a dated curation audit of
public social-services listings for Snyder County, PA (a rural county
in central Pennsylvania; county seat Middleburg; largest town
Selinsgrove; other towns Shamokin Dam, Freeburg, Port Trevorton,
McClure, Beaver Springs, Richfield, Mount Pleasant Mills,
Kratzerville, Paxtonville, Beavertown, Hummels Wharf), checked against
the organisations' own websites and other public listings. It is not
a replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open
services, and it should not be used as one without checking with the
organisation or PA 211 first.

Each category folder holds three files: a README with the headline
corrections, the full audit report with evidence and sources, and the
original pilot list it was checked against.

The auditor is NOT a local resident of Snyder County. This audit was
prepared with assistance from an AI assistant, Muse. Every report
documents its provenance and limits; nothing was invented, imputed, or
deleted silently, and no organisation is called closed or defunct from
a failed page load — anything not confirmed from the organisation's
own site is marked UNVERIFIED.

## Honest limit — read this first
PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven, and a text fetch could
not pull a complete Snyder County-filtered list, so these are partial
pilots anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists and
the organisations' own websites — NOT complete county directories.
**A live-browser check of search.pa211.org filtered to Snyder County
is still outstanding before any send.** Each report carries a pre-send
re-check list. Several category checks also hit a 429 rate limit on
page-text fetches late in the day, so some own-site claims rest on
search-returned own-site text rather than full page fetches; those
items are flagged in their reports and must be re-checked live before
any send.

## Headline corrections (one per category)
- **Food:** Loaves and Fishes, Selinsgrove, distributes last Friday
  12:30–2:00pm, not 4th Thursday; Five Barley Loaves is in McClure
  (6 W Specht St), not Beaver Springs; Union-Snyder CAA's Emergency
  Food Program and Emergency Food Clearinghouse are one service
  (713 Bridge St, Selinsgrove, 570-374-0181) listed twice; nine
  "Snyder County" listings are actually in Union/Northumberland
  Counties; no Salvation Army pantry was found in Snyder County.
- **Shelters:** Snyder County has no general emergency shelter
  building of its own — PA 211 "Overnight Shelters" hits resolve to
  Coordinated Entry/intake at Union-Snyder CAA, 713 Bridge St,
  Selinsgrove, not shelter beds. Transitions of PA is confirmed by
  hotline only (1-800-850-7948); its shelter location is
  confidential. "Susquehanna Valley Women in Transition" is
  Transitions of PA's former name, not a second shelter. The closest
  general emergency shelter serving Snyder residents is Haven
  Ministry, Sunbury (Northumberland County).
- **Mental health / crisis:** The county crisis line is **CMSU
  TAPline, 1-800-222-9016, 24/7** — do not use (570) 275-4962 as a
  crisis number (that is CMSU's Danville admin/referral line). PA 211
  appears to list Beacon Light CSRU's fax number, (570) 415-0511,
  as its voice number; the voice number is 570-415-0510. No walk-in
  crisis centre was found inside Snyder County.
- **Housing:** ERAP is stale and should be removed from live help
  (ERAP1 ended 2022-09-30, ERAP2 ended 2025-09-30). The Snyder
  County Assistance Office is at **83 Maple Lane, Selinsgrove,
  570-374-8126** — retire 570 South High Street. The Snyder County
  Housing Authority is at **106 Drake Court, Middleburg,
  570-837-3979**, not 103 Drake Court; its Section 8 waitlist status
  is unresolved across sources, so neither open nor closed is
  published. PAHAF is closed (no new applications).
- **Utilities:** LIHEAP's 2026–27 season is open (Nov 2, 2026 –
  Apr 23, 2027); LIHWAP is closed (federal programme ended
  2023–24). The County Assistance Office is at 83 Maple Lane,
  Selinsgrove, 570-374-8126 — PPL's stale LIHEAP PDF lists the
  Schuylkill number instead. PPL CARES/OnTrack is 1-800-342-5775,
  not (800) 358-6623 (the payment-arrangements line). UGI serves
  only part of Snyder County, so UGI CAP/Operation Share/LIURP are
  not countywide; PPL serves all 21 municipalities.
- **Employment:** No walk-in PA CareerLink exists in Snyder County;
  service arrives via The Link mobile unit on a monthly schedule
  and the Sunbury office (225 Market St, Sunbury, 570-988-7300).
  The CSIU LPN Career Center has moved to Milton; PA 211 still
  lists the Lewisburg address. Snyder's OVR is Williamsport (and
  Altoona for blindness/visual), not Harrisburg.
- **Child care:** Snyder County is ELRC **Region 8** (a conflicting
  Regions 9 & 10 claim is flagged for pre-send confirmation, not
  treated as a second ELRC). SUMMIT Susquehanna Center, Selinsgrove,
  is at 550 University Ave, not 590. The CONNECT Helpline covers
  birth–5, not birth–3 only (1-800-692-7288).
- **Seniors:** No AAA office in Middleburg — Union-Snyder AAA is at
  116 N. 2nd St, Lewisburg (Union County), 570-524-2100; the Snyder
  line is 570-374-5558. PA 211's PA MEDI record still says
  "APPRISE" — stale name. Only three senior centres serve Snyder
  County (Selinsgrove, Penns Creek, Beaver Springs).
- **Disability:** Snyder's OVR is Williamsport BVRS and Altoona
  BBVS; Harrisburg BBVS does not serve Snyder. Snyder's Center for
  Independent Living is Roads to Freedom, Williamsport, not
  Harrisburg. North Central Sight Services does not serve Snyder;
  the Snyder-area sight agency is Central Susquehanna Sight
  Services, Sunbury, (570) 286-1471.
- **Substance use:** Conewago Snyder residential is men 18+ only,
  28 beds — not co-ed / 42-bed as aggregators say. No separate
  "Union-Snyder Drug & Alcohol" office exists — the Single County
  Authority is CMSU. Walking Free Clubhouse (8183 Route 522,
  Middleburg, opened Jan 2025) is the county's peer-recovery space
  and was not in the retrievable PA 211 items.
- **Legal aid:** MidPenn Legal Services does NOT serve Snyder
  County; the correct provider is North Penn Legal Services,
  Sunbury office. Summit Legal Aid does not serve Snyder either.
  The PBA Lawyer Referral Service is a PAID referral ($30 max for
  a 30-minute consult), not free aid, and its current number is
  800-932-0311 option 2.
- **Transportation:** The shared-ride operator and MATP
  administrator is **rabbittransit (SRTA), scheduling
  1-800-632-9063** — "Union-Snyder Transportation Alliance" is a
  stale name (merged 2016). MATP is NOT STEP, Inc. Shared-ride
  fares have risen (senior $3.00–$7.50, general public $20–$50 by
  zone). Greyhound Selinsgrove is a Fullington Trailways flag stop
  at 105 S. Market St, not a Greyhound station; there is no
  fixed-route bus in Snyder County.

## What this audit does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. Items
  that could not be confirmed from an organisation's own site are
  marked UNVERIFIED, not closed.
- It does not claim to be a complete directory of Snyder County
  services. See "Honest limit" above.
- Bed availability, waitlist positions, and seasonal status are
  date-stamped to 2026-11-15 and change constantly; check with the
  organisation before relying on them.
- Fares and schedules are date-stamped to 2026-11-15.
- It does not rewrite expert advisory content (medical, mental-health,
  substance-use, or legal advice). Only contact information,
  structure, and metadata were audited.
