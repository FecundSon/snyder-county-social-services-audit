## How to read this file

- "PA 211 claim" = what the PA 211 record / search snippet said, where a PA 211 record was retrievable. Where no PA 211 record was retrievable, the claim source is named (e.g. CAA directory, operator site, carrier site, aggregator).
- Addresses are recorded as found; county scope was judged by exact address/service area, never by ZIP or town name alone. Lewisburg, Milton, Mifflinburg and Lewistown are in other counties and are OUT OF SCOPE as locations, even when a service based there serves Snyder County residents (that distinction is recorded per row).
