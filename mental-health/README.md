## Headline corrections

1. **Beacon Light CSRU, McClure — PA 211 appears to list the fax number as the voice number.** PA 211 gives (570) 415-0511; Beacon Light's own site gives phone **570-415-0510** and fax 570-415-0511. Correction: voice 570-415-0510.
2. **Geisinger Behavioral Health Center Danville — 570-271-6211 is the wrong number for this facility.** That is Geisinger Medical Center's main number. The provider's own site lists Danville admissions as **(844) 758-1164**; aggregators variously list (570) 271-9200 and (570) 953-8552, so the admissions number needs a live re-check before any send.
3. **Gaudenzia Middleburg Outpatient — the local number is 570-837-2208.** Recovery.com shows (877) 706-7641 (national intake); Gaudenzia's own place data shows a Harrisburg-area 717 number. CARF and multiple directories agree on **570-837-2208** for the Middleburg clinic.

## County crisis line

**CMSU TAPline: 1-800-222-9016, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week**, held by CMSU Behavioral Health & Developmental Services (Columbia, Montour, Snyder and Union Counties). Confirmed on CMSU's own crisis page, the PA DHS county crisis list, and the PA Department of Education county contacts page. It is separate from 988 (national Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) — the two must not be substituted for each other. Do not use (570) 275-4962 as a crisis number; that is CMSU's Danville administrative/referral line.

## Key clarifications

- **No walk-in crisis centre was found inside Snyder County.** The nearest 24/7 walk-in mental-health assessment found is Geisinger Behavioral Health Center, Danville (Montour County).
- **CMSU's LOSS Team does not do direct outreach in Snyder County** (Columbia and Montour Counties only, per its own page).
- **NAMI Susquehanna Valley has no confirmed Snyder County meeting** — office in Bloomsburg (Columbia County); support groups listed as "Coming Soon".
- **"Middleburg" is two places:** True North Psychological Services is Middleburg, **Virginia** (out of scope); Gaudenzia Middleburg Outpatient is Middleburg, **PA** (in scope).
- **Selinsgrove Center is not a public mental-health clinic** — it is a state residential ICF/ID facility; psychiatric services are for residents only.
- **Selinsgrove REC is a community wellness centre, not a clinical provider.**

## Method and limits

Public web only — search and page-text fetch; nobody contacted. Pilot of 28 listings built from retrievable PA 211 records, the Union–Snyder Community Action Agency *Human Services* directory (July 2025), the Susquehanna Kids mental-health guide, PA DHS / PA Department of Education county lists, and organisations' own sites. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. Every street address was verified by exact geocoding against county boundaries.

**Limit encountered on 2026-11-15:** after four page fetches the page-text service rate-limited further opens (429), so four Selinsgrove private practices (Courtyard Counseling, Step By Step Counseling, Mid River Counseling, Hempfield Behavioral Services) could not be checked against their own sites and are marked UNVERIFIED — not confirmed, and not called closed or defunct. Their addresses geocode to Snyder County; phones/hours rest on directory search results only.

No crisis or treatment advice is given anywhere in this audit; contact information only. No clinician's personal phone is published. Waitlist, capacity, insurance and seasonal status were not evidenced for any listing.
