**Auditor:** not a local resident of Snyder County. Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).

> **HONEST LIMIT:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Snyder County check is outstanding before any send.

## How this list was built

PA 211's county-filtered search could not be pulled in full by text fetch (JavaScript-driven pages). The pilot was therefore built from: (a) retrievable PA 211 record pages found by search; (b) the Union–Snyder Community Action Agency *Human Services* directory (July 2025 edition); (c) the Susquehanna Kids mental-health resources guide (CMSU SAP liaison lists); (d) the PA Department of Education / PACAMHDS county contacts page and the PA DHS county crisis-numbers list; (e) organisations' own sites found by search. Every address below was checked by exact geocoding (see audit report); towns in other counties — Lewisburg, Milton, Mifflinburg, Lewistown, Danville, Sunbury, Bloomsburg, Wilkes-Barre — are flagged where they appear.

No organisation was contacted. No crisis or treatment advice is given anywhere in this audit; contact information only.

## Notes on this pilot list

- **No walk-in crisis centre was found inside Snyder County.** The nearest 24/7 walk-in mental-health assessment found is Geisinger Behavioral Health Center, Danville (Montour County) — see audit report.
- **Two "Middleburg" traps were caught by geocoding:** True North Psychological Services is Middleburg, **Virginia** (row 24, out of scope); Gaudenzia Middleburg Outpatient is Middleburg, **PA** (row 22, in scope).
- **Private practices (rows 18–21):** listed as publicly listed practices only. No clinician's personal phone is published here beyond a publicly listed practice number. Their own sites could not be opened on 2026-11-15 (browser rate limit, 429); they are marked UNVERIFIED in the audit report, not confirmed.
- **Laurel Life / Folium Inc.** (550 Shermans Valley Road, New Bloomfield, Perry County) appears in aggregator lists for the Selinsgrove area; it is outside Snyder County and was not given a pilot row — it would be out of scope on address.
- **Language access:** CCBH lists a Spanish line (866-229-3187). No other listing in this pilot states language access on its own site in the sources checked; that is a gap to re-check, not a finding that none exists.
