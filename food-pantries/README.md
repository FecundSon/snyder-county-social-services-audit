# Snyder County, PA — Food Pantry Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-15. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211, the Central Pennsylvania Food Bank,
Union-Snyder Community Action Agency, or any pantry. No organisation was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 17 in-scope food pantry / food distribution
listings for Snyder County, PA (county seat Middleburg; largest town
Selinsgrove; other towns Shamokin Dam, Freeburg, Port Trevorton,
McClure, Beaver Springs, Richfield, Mount Pleasant Mills, Kratzerville,
Paxtonville, Beavertown, Hummels Wharf), plus 9 out-of-county
exclusions, checked against the pantries' own websites and other
public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory:
it does not claim to be a current list of open pantries, and it should
not be used as one without checking with the pantry or PA 211 first.
The auditor is NOT a local resident of Snyder County.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. **Loaves and Fishes, St. Paul's UCC, 400 N Market St, Selinsgrove**
   — the distribution is on the **last Friday of the month,
   12:30–2:00pm**, not the 4th Thursday with a 3:00–5:30pm second
   session given by the Central Pennsylvania Food Bank map as
   republished. CAA's own 2020/2021 schedules, the Agency on Aging
   2025 guide, and St. Paul's own 2026 volunteer days (Friday,
   October 30 and Friday, November 27) all say Friday. For Snyder
   County residents east of Rt. 104.
2. **Grace Works / Grace Food Pantry, Grace Covenant Community
   Church, Middleburg** — Monday hours are now **9:00–11:00am**,
   not 10:00–11:00am (the church's own flyer, posted November 9,
   2026; closed November 26 for Thanksgiving). Wednesday 5–6pm is
   unchanged in the evidence found. Open to Snyder County residents.
3. **Five Barley Loaves, McClure** — it is in **McClure, at 6 W
   Specht St** (Trinity Lutheran parish hall, corner of Specht &
   Zeller), **not Beaver Springs**, and its hours are **10:00–
   11:00am on the last Friday** (Trinity Lutheran's own site),
   not 10am–12pm or 10:00–11:30am. November and December move to
   the Friday before Thanksgiving and Christmas. For Snyder County
   residents west of Rt. 104.
4. **Martha's Table, All Saints Episcopal Church, 129 N Market St,
   Selinsgrove** — a **free community meal, not a grocery
   pantry** (1st, 3rd and 5th Wednesdays except December, doors
   4:30pm, dinner until 5:30pm or the food is gone), and the phone
   is **(570) 374-8289** (own site and PA 211), not
   (570) 374-9872.
5. **Union-Snyder CAA, 713 Bridge St, Suite 10, Selinsgrove** —
   PA 211 lists this service twice, as "Emergency Food Program"
   and "Emergency Food Clearinghouse". It is **one service, one
   office, one phone — (570) 374-0181**, Mon/Wed/Thu/Fri
   8:30am–4:00pm, Tue 9:30am–4:00pm. List it once. It is emergency
   food between scheduled pantries, not a scheduled weekly pantry.
6. **Brookside Ministries Church, 11 Stetler Ave, Shamokin Dam** —
   the published "Sunday–Thursday, 9:00am–12:00pm" pantry hours
   **could not be verified as pantry hours**; no pantry schedule
   appears on the church's own site, and the hours resemble
   church/office hours. Address and phone (570) 743-5683 are
   confirmed; the pantry schedule is UNVERIFIED.
7. **MilitaryShare, Post 25, 524 S Market St, Selinsgrove** — a
   distribution for **military households only, pre-registration
   required** (call 570.541.5155 for the schedule). It is not a
   general walk-in pantry.
8. **Helping Hands Helping Others, Beaver Lutheran Church, Rt. 522,
   Beavertown** — a **monthly** distribution, **3rd Tuesday from
   10:00am** (church's own newsletter), not a weekly pantry. Not
   government funded, so no income limits.
9. **Several listings on "Snyder County" food lists are in Union or
   Northumberland County and are OUT OF SCOPE** — Love in Action
   (New Columbia, Union), Lewisburg Food Pantry, Mifflinburg YMCA
   mobile distribution, Harvest Time and Beacon Ministries
   (Lewisburg, Union), the CAA Food Hub at the Miller Center
   (Lewisburg, Union), Elijah's Bowl (Sunbury, Northumberland),
   Kate's Kupboard (Northumberland, Northumberland), and the
   Salvation Army listing (Sunbury, Northumberland). **No Salvation
   Army corps or pantry in Snyder County was found at all.**

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-15. No pantry,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- A pantry's own website (including its own Facebook page) is treated
  as authoritative over third-party directories. Where a pantry's own
  site did not state an address or hours, that item is marked
  UNVERIFIED in the full report — it is a gap in public web evidence,
  not a finding, and must not be published as fact.
- **No pantry is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed or
  missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a pantry is currently
  operating.
- Hours from a church/office/thrift page are not pantry hours, and
  are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible (notably
  Brookside Ministries).
- Monthly distributions are not weekly pantries, and meals are not
  grocery pantries; both distinctions are flagged wherever they
  applied (Loaves & Fishes, Five Barley Loaves, Helping Hands,
  Bread of Life, Christ's Prayer Pantry, Martha's Table, Shepherd's
  Pie, Meals on Wheels, MilitaryShare).
- Every address was verified by exact geocoding (full street
  address), never by ZIP or town name alone. ZIP code does not
  settle county in this area: several pantries that appear on
  "near Snyder County" lists are in fact in Union County
  (Lewisburg, Mifflinburg, New Columbia) or Northumberland County
  (Sunbury, Northumberland). They are listed as OUT OF SCOPE
  exclusions, not as Snyder County pantries.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-
  driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-15 returned only retrievable
  individual PA 211 items — no complete extractable Snyder County
  result list. The pilot is therefore anchored on those retrievable
  PA 211 items (Union-Snyder CAA Emergency Food Program /
  Clearinghouse, Martha's Table, Selinsgrove Area Meals on Wheels),
  the Central Pennsylvania Food Bank partner list as republished by
  HelpByState (last checked October 9, 2026), Union-Snyder CAA's own
  food page and 2020/2021 pantry schedules, the Union-Snyder Agency
  on Aging Older Adult Resources guide (May 30, 2025), the Episcopal
  Diocese of Central Pennsylvania food list, Snyder County Times
  community-calendar notices, and the organisations' own sites,
  each item labelled with its claim source. A live-browser check of
  search.pa211.org for Snyder County is still outstanding before any
  send to PA 211.
- Holiday hours matter in this county and are date-stamped in the
  full report: Grace Works closed November 26, 2026; Five Barley
  Loaves moves to the Friday before Thanksgiving and Christmas;
  Martha's Table does not run in December; Bread of Life and
  Christ's Prayer Pantry shift their Saturday dates around holidays.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the pantries themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
