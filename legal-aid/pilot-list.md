## Geocoding / scope notes (as found)

- **Sunbury (Northumberland County), Lewisburg (Union County), Bloomsburg (Columbia County), Harrisburg (Dauphin County) and Philadelphia are OUT of Snyder County.** Listings based there are in scope only where the organisation's own site states it serves Snyder County residents (e.g. North Penn Sunbury office; Transitions of PA; statewide helplines). An address in another county is not, by itself, a reason to exclude a listing — service area is the test.
- **Lewisburg, Milton, Mifflinburg and Lewistown are in other counties and are OUT OF SCOPE as Snyder locations.** No listing was treated as a Snyder location on the basis of ZIP or town name alone.
- **MidPenn Legal Services** was included in the pilot because Justia lists it for Snyder County; its service area was to be verified, not assumed.
- **Summit Legal Aid** was included because it appeared in a retrievable PA 211 search result for a Snyder County query; its service area was to be verified, not assumed.
