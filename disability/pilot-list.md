## Honest limit

PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Snyder County check is outstanding before any send.

## County rule applied while building the list

Every address was checked by exact geocoding, never by ZIP or town name alone. Lewisburg (Union County), Milton and Sunbury (Northumberland County), Lewistown (Mifflin County) and Mifflinburg (Union County) are OUT OF COUNTY as office locations; offices there are kept only where they are the serving office for Snyder residents, and are labelled as such. Geocoding done 2026-11-15: 1083 Bloom Road, Danville = Montour County; 90 Lawton Lane, Milton = Northumberland County; 326 Market Street, Sunbury = Northumberland County; 24 East Third Street, Williamsport = Lycoming County; 1130 12th Avenue, Altoona = Blair County; 1372 North Susquehanna Trail, Selinsgrove = Snyder County.
