# Snyder County, PA — Shelters Audit (README)

**County:** Snyder County, Pennsylvania (county seat Middleburg; largest
town Selinsgrove). Rural central Pennsylvania.
**Category:** Shelters (emergency, family, youth, veterans).
**Directory checked against:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Date checked:** 2026-11-15.
**Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide
PA 211, to any shelter, or to any other directory. No organisation was
contacted.**

**Auditor:** Keith Conklin. Keith is NOT a local resident of Snyder County.
This audit was prepared with assistance from his AI assistant, Muse.

## Headline corrections

1. **Snyder County has no general emergency shelter building of its own.**
   The PA 211 "Overnight Shelters" items that resolve to Snyder County are
   Coordinated Entry / assessment records at the Union-Snyder Community
   Action Agency office (713 Bridge St, Suite 10, Selinsgrove) — intake and
   rehousing help, not shelter beds. Directories should present that address
   as a Coordinated Entry access site (Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:00pm, walk-ins
   accepted, phone intake accepted, (570) 374-0181, or call 2-1-1), never as
   an emergency shelter address.
2. **Snyder County Assistance Office address is wrong in older listings.**
   Current address: **83 Maple Lane, Selinsgrove, PA 17870,
   (570) 374-8126**, toll-free 1-866-713-8584. Older aggregators still show
   570 S High St, Selinsgrove — that should be corrected.
3. **Transitions of PA (domestic-violence emergency shelter, serving Snyder
   County) is confirmed by hotline only: 24-hour hotline 1-800-850-7948,
   Snyder County office (570) 374-7773.** Its shelter is in a confidential
   location; no shelter address is published in these files, and PA 211
   should list the record by hotline only (or clearly as an office/intake
   point), never as a shelter address.
4. **"Susquehanna Valley Women in Transition" is not a second shelter — it
   is Transitions of PA's former name** (same phones: (570) 374-7773 and
   1-800-850-7948). Directories carrying both names double-count one
   organisation.
5. **For Snyder County veterans, the SSVF provider is the Veterans
   Multi-Service Center (844-226-0368), not the Veterans Leadership
   Program.** VLP's own county list omits Snyder County. No PA 211 record
   claiming VLP serves Snyder was found, so this is a guardrail for future
   listings, not a found PA 211 error.
6. **By Grace Women's Home (Middleburg) cannot be confirmed as open, and
   its phone number conflicts** — (570) 259-6132 in a 2018 directory vs
   (570) 765-7301 in a CareerLink list; the organisation's own Facebook
   posts (Sep 2026) said it was preparing to open in October 2026. Neither
   number is verified, and no street address is published (none should be
   added).
7. **The closest general emergency shelter serving Snyder residents is
   Haven Ministry, Sunbury — in Northumberland County, not Snyder County**
   (1043 S Front St, Sunbury, (570) 286-1672). Do not list it as a Snyder
   County shelter; label it Sunbury / Northumberland County.
8. **No youth shelter and no winter/warming overnight shelter serve Snyder
   County.** No dedicated youth emergency shelter and no Code Blue overnight
   shelter were found for the county; the only in-county warming provision
   found is daytime warming/cooling at Snyder County Libraries, Selinsgrove.
   These are gaps in provision, not listing errors.
9. **There is no Salvation Army shelter in Snyder County.** The nearest
   Corps are Sunbury and Milton (both Northumberland County), and neither
   has an on-site shelter confirmed on the Salvation Army's own site.

## Method and limits

- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-15. No shelter, no
  directory, and no one else was contacted.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Items that could not be confirmed from an
  organisation's own site are marked CLARIFIED or UNVERIFIED — a gap in
  public web evidence, not a finding.
- **Direct page-text fetch was rate-limited on 2026-11-15** (repeated
  `browser.open` calls returned an upstream 429 and were not retried), so
  own-site claims rest on the organisations' own pages as returned by
  search, plus PA 211 records and the Inglis/PHFA Snyder County guide. A
  live-browser re-check of the own sites (and of PA 211) is outstanding
  before any send.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed.** A failed or missing page
  is reported as unverified, never as a closure.
- **Bed availability is never published as a static fact.** Only intake
  phones and intake processes are reported.
- **Confidential locations:** 2 items are counted only / listed by town and
  phone only — Transitions of PA (domestic-violence shelter, confidential
  location) and By Grace Women's Home (women's transitional home, street
  address not published). No address for either appears in these files.
- **Honest limit:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text
  fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial
  pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus
  organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser
  PA 211 Snyder County check is outstanding before any send.
- Every in-county address was verified by exact geocoding, not ZIP code:
  713 Bridge St, Selinsgrove, 83 Maple Ln, Selinsgrove, and 106 Drake Ct,
  Middleburg, all geocode to Snyder County; Haven Ministry (Sunbury) to
  Northumberland; the Gate House (Danville) to Montour; Shelter Services
  (Lewistown) to Mifflin; Housing Transitions (State College) to Centre;
  VMC Lock Haven to Clinton.
