# Snyder County, PA — Employment / Job Help Audit Report
Date checked: 2026-11-15
County: Snyder County, PA (county seat Middleburg; largest town
Selinsgrove). Category: employment / job help.
Method: pilot list built from public directories (statewide PA 211
retrievable entries, Advance Central PA own site, PA OVR directory,
Union-Snyder CAA Human Services directory July 2025, Union & Snyder
County Reentry Coalition guide June 2025); every item then checked
against the organisation's own website/page, via public web search and
page-text fetches. Own site beats directory; directory beats a
third-party aggregator. County for every address was verified by exact
geocoding, never by ZIP or town name alone.
Status: NOT SENT YET. Nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211
or to any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any
outward report. This audit does not refer to Keith as a local
resident of Snyder County.
Recipient note: the directory is statewide PA 211 (pa211.org /
search.pa211.org) — NOT PA 211 East and NOT the Lancaster database
manager. Snyder County sits in Advance Central PA's nine-county area
(Centre, Clinton, Columbia, Lycoming, Mifflin, Montour,
Northumberland, Snyder, Union), which is why regional listings
routinely mix counties.

## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site evidence, material to someone trying
to use the service. All other items are in the per-listing table below.

1. **There is no walk-in PA CareerLink office in Snyder County.
   Snyder County's CareerLink is in Sunbury, Northumberland County.**
   Advance Central PA's own locations page (fetched 2026-11-15) lists
   six PA CareerLink offices and none is in Snyder County. The office
   named for Snyder is PA CareerLink Northumberland / Snyder / Union
   Counties, 225 Market St, Sunbury, PA 17801, 570-988-7300,
   TTY 570-286-8293, Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri 8:30am–4:30pm, Wed 9:00am–4:30pm.
   Geocoding confirms 225 Market St, Sunbury is Northumberland County.
   Any listing that gives a Snyder County walk-in CareerLink address
   is wrong.
2. **The old Selinsgrove CareerLink is gone from the current list —
   do not list 713 Bridge St, Suite 11, Selinsgrove as a CareerLink.**
   A 2009 Team PA list gave a Selinsgrove CareerLink at 713 Bridge St,
   Suite 11, 570-374-5751. Advance Central PA's current six-office
   list has no Selinsgrove office. 713 Bridge St, Selinsgrove (geocoded:
   Snyder County) is now the Union-Snyder Community Action Agency
   (Suite 10) and a stop for The Link mobile unit — not a CareerLink
   office. The same applies to the old Lewisburg CareerLink in that
   2009 list: no Lewisburg office appears on Advance Central PA's
   current locations page.
3. **In Snyder County, CareerLink comes to the job seeker on The Link
   mobile unit — list the schedule, not a fixed office.**
   The Link is Advance Central PA's ADA-accessible mobile career centre
   (computers, internet, printing, job matching, career guidance,
   workforce training). Its November 2026 schedule shows Snyder stops
   at Snyder County Courthouse, 9 W Market St, Middleburg, on 11/3
   (10:00am–2:00pm) and at the Union-Snyder CAA, 713 Bridge St,
   Selinsgrove, on 11/19 (10:00am–2:00pm); other months show Rudy
   Gelnett Memorial Library, 1 N High St, Selinsgrove. Coordinator:
   Robert A. Eddy, 570-220-0003, reddy@censop.com. A listing that
   gives The Link a fixed address or fixed weekly hours misdescribes it.
4. **CSIU LPN Career Center has moved — PA 211's Lewisburg address
   is stale.** PA 211 lists the Central Susquehanna LPN Career Center
   at 1339 St Mary St, Suite 2, Lewisburg, PA 17837, 570-768-4960.
   The centre's own site (lpn.csiu.org) describes it as "newly
   relocated in Milton, Pa." at 15 Lawton Ln, Milton, with the same
   phone 570-768-4960. Geocoding confirms 15 Lawton Ln, Milton is
   Northumberland County (and 1339 St Mary St, Lewisburg is Union
   County). The Lewisburg address should not be published as current;
   confirm the Milton address in the live re-check before sending.
5. **CSO is not Snyder County's Community Action Agency — do not
   merge it with Union-Snyder CAA.** Central Susquehanna Opportunities,
   Inc. (CSO), 2 E Arch St, Suite 313, Shamokin, PA 17872,
   570-644-6575 (geocoded: Northumberland County), is the Community
   Action Agency for Columbia, Montour, and Northumberland Counties
   per PA 211, and runs EARN employment workshops at the Sunbury
   CareerLink. Snyder County's Community Action Agency is the
   Union-Snyder CAA, 713 Bridge St, Suite 10, Selinsgrove,
   570-374-0181. Two different agencies; listing CSO as a Snyder
   County agency misdirects people.
6. **Union-Snyder CAA employment help is referral-based, not a
   general walk-in job centre.** The CAA's own directory and the
   Reentry Coalition guide list Employment and Work Ready Programs
   (Outreach Services and Supported Work components, Supported
   Engagement Program) at 713 Bridge St, Suite 10, Selinsgrove,
   570-374-0181 — but Work Ready is for TANF participants and needs a
   referral through the County Assistance Office. Do not present it as
   open walk-in job help.
7. **OVR for Snyder County is Williamsport (general) and Altoona
   (blindness/visual) — not Harrisburg.** Per the pa.gov OVR office
   directory, Snyder is served by the Williamsport BVRS district
   office, The Grit Building, Suite 102, 208 W 3rd St, Williamsport,
   PA 17701, 570-327-3600, TTY 570-327-3620, toll-free 1-800-442-6359
   (geocoded: Lycoming County), and for blindness/visual services by
   Altoona BBVS, 1130 12th Ave, Suite 300, Altoona, PA 16601,
   814-946-7330, toll-free 1-866-695-7673. A Harrisburg OVR address
   for Snyder would be the wrong district.
8. **SUN Tech is a Union County school, not a Snyder County one —
   list it as serving Snyder students/adults, never as a Snyder site.**
   SUN Area Technical Institute, 815 E Market St, New Berlin,
   PA 17855, 570-966-1031 (geocoded: Union County), serves high school
   students from Selinsgrove and Midd-West districts (PA 211 student
   programme: seniors only, service area Union County) and offers
   adult day/evening programmes (570-966-1031 x125). Its address must
   not be presented as in Snyder County.

## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. Susquehanna
  Valley Mediation's employment help, a separate Experience Works
  SCSEP listing, and North Central Sight Services' Snyder employment
  service are UNVERIFIED from own sites in these searches — that is a
  gap in public web evidence, not a finding.
- It does not claim the old Selinsgrove or Lewisburg CareerLinks are
  "closed" as organisations — only that they do not appear on Advance
  Central PA's current six-office list, so they must not be published
  as current walk-in CareerLink offices. The live re-check should
  confirm this before any send.
- It does not claim a programme exists in Snyder County because a
  directory lists it for Snyder. Most employment help for Snyder
  residents is delivered from offices in neighbouring counties
  (Sunbury, Williamsport, Shamokin, Milton, Lewisburg, New Berlin,
  Altoona) or by a mobile unit; the report says so for each item
  rather than implying a Snyder walk-in office.
- It does not publish a funder/operator as a second centre (Advance
  Central PA), a class site as a headquarters (CSIU adult-education
  sites), a regional HQ as a local office (CSO Shamokin; AHEDD Camp
  Hill), or a narrow programme as general help (CAA Work Ready is
  TANF/referral-based; SCSEP is 55+; YES to the Future is 16–24;
  SUN Tech student training is seniors only).
- It does not settle county from ZIP code or town name. Sunbury,
  Shamokin, Milton, Lewisburg, New Berlin, Williamsport, Lewistown
  and Bloomsburg addresses were each checked by exact geocoding
  (see pilot-list.md).
- It does not give employment or benefits advice. It audits contact
  information only: names, addresses, phones, hours, and intake
  routes as organisations themselves state them.
- It gives no street address for any domestic-violence safe house.
  No DV service is in this category, and none was needed.
- Three page-text fetches in this run hit a 429 rate limit and were
  not retried; no finding depends on a retried fetch (see Evidence
  note above). The LPN relocation and old-CareerLink findings rest on
  own-site pages as returned in search results plus the successfully
  fetched Advance Central PA locations page, and are flagged for
  live confirmation before any send.
