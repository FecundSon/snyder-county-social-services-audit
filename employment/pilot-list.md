# Snyder County, PA — Employment / Job Help Audit Pilot
Date started: 2026-11-15 — AUDIT COMPLETE, awaiting Keith's accept checkpoint.
Scope: job centres, career training, resume/interview help, vocational
rehabilitation, and supported employment serving Snyder County, PA
(county seat Middleburg; largest town Selinsgrove; other towns Shamokin
Dam, Freeburg, Port Trevorton, McClure, Beaver Springs, Richfield,
Mount Pleasant Mills, Kratzerville, Paxtonville, Beavertown, Hummels
Wharf). This file is the candidate list from public directories;
findings are in audit-report-2026-11-15.md (same folder). Nothing has
been sent to PA 211 or any organisation. The auditor is NOT a local
resident of Snyder County. Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).

## Honest limit
PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull
a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on
retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own
sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Snyder
County check is outstanding before any send.

## Rules applied to this list
- No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- Own site beats directory. Directory beats a third-party aggregator.
- No claim that an organisation is closed/defunct is made from a failed
  page load; such items are UNVERIFIED.
- A funder/operator name is not a second walk-in centre; a programme
  listed under a funder's name rather than the provider's name is a
  duplicate risk, flagged, not double-counted.
- Commercial staffing/recruitment firms are NOT counted as public
  employment help (see Excluded section).
- County is settled by exact-address geocoding, never by ZIP or town
  name alone. Lewisburg, Milton, Mifflinburg, Lewistown, State College
  are in other counties and are OUT OF SCOPE as sites, even when they
  serve Snyder residents.
- No safe-house or confidential location is in this file.
- This audit does not refer to Keith as a local resident of Snyder County.

## Excluded from this employment audit (not public employment help)
- Commercial staffing/recruitment firms with Selinsgrove/Sunbury
  offices (e.g. One Source Staffing Solutions, 1969 N Susquehanna
  Trail, Selinsgrove; Spherion) — employers/recruiters, not free
  public employment-help services. Not counted, not presented as
  PA 211-style help.
- Individual employers' hiring pages and job boards (Indeed,
  LinkedIn, Career.io postings) — not services. Selinsgrove Center
  (a large Snyder County employer) hiring posts are employer hiring,
  not job help.
- The Union-Snyder CAA Human Services directory itself (July 2025)
  and the Reentry Coalition guide (June 2025) are candidate sources,
  not current directories; their entries were re-checked elsewhere
  or marked UNVERIFIED.
