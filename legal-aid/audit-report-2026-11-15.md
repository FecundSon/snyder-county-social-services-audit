## Headline findings (only own-site-evidenced, material corrections)

1. **MidPenn Legal Services does NOT serve Snyder County — the correct regional civil legal-aid provider is North Penn Legal Services, Sunbury office.** MidPenn's own locations/services pages list 18 counties — Adams, Bedford, Berks, Blair, Centre, Clearfield, Cumberland, Dauphin, Franklin, Fulton, Huntingdon, Juniata, Lancaster, Lebanon, Mifflin, Perry, Schuylkill and York — Snyder is not among them. Justia's Snyder listing of MidPenn (Harrisburg office, (717) 234-0492) is therefore misleading for Snyder residents.
2. **Summit Legal Aid does NOT serve Snyder County.** Its own service description covers ten western/southwestern counties (Armstrong, Cambria, Clarion, Fayette, Greene, Indiana, Jefferson, Somerset, Washington, Westmoreland). Its appearance in a PA 211 Snyder query result is a directory-matching artefact, not a Snyder service.
3. **The Pennsylvania Bar Association Lawyer Referral Service is a PAID referral, not free legal aid — and its current number is 800-932-0311 (select option 2), not 800-692-7375.** PBA's own Find a Lawyer page states the initial 30-minute consultation costs no more than $30, and that LRS staff are not attorneys and cannot provide legal advice. The 800-692-7375 number still shown on North Penn's Snyder local-resources page is an older PBA LRS number; the current PBA page gives 800-932-0311, option 2.
4. **Pennsylvania SeniorLAW Helpline hours are Monday–Thursday 10:00 AM–12:00 PM on SeniorLAW Center's own current pages — not 10:00 AM–2:00 PM as stated in several older secondary sources.** The 10–2 window appears in older SeniorLAW pages and in a national senior-helpline directory; the Center's current Helpline, Access Services and Victim Services pages all state 10 AM–12 PM, Mon–Thu. The helpline number 1-877-727-7529 and the 60+ age scope are confirmed.
5. **North Penn Legal Services intake hours are not office hours, and there is no Friday intake.** The Sunbury office's own/PALawHELP intake is Mon–Thu 9:00–11:30 AM and 1:30–4:00 PM (PALawHELP text also gives a 4:30 PM close in one place — see row 1); online intake is available anytime. The office's general opening hours (8:30 AM–4:30 PM, Mon–Fri, per directory listings) must not be presented as intake hours.
6. **Transitions of PA's main office is in Lewisburg (Union County), not Snyder County — but it does serve Snyder County.** Its own site states it is the only comprehensive crime victim centre serving Union, Snyder and Northumberland Counties, with a 24-hour hotline 1-800-850-7948. Any PA 211 record giving a Lewisburg address for a Snyder service is correct as a service-area record, not as a Snyder location.
