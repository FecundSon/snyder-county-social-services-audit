## Headline corrections

1. **Conewago Snyder (Firetree, Beavertown) residential treatment is for men aged 18+ only, 28 beds — not co-ed, not 42 beds as aggregators say.** The detox unit on the same campus has accepted women since 1 Oct 2023; residential and detox must not be conflated.
2. **CMSU's PA 211 listing implies CMSU directly provides inpatient detox, inpatient rehab and a halfway house; CMSU's own site says its case managers assess and refer, and CMSU contracts providers for the continuum of care.** Address (858 Sale Barn Rd, Middleburg), phone (570-837-3102) and intake route are confirmed; the service list needs qualifying.
3. **There is no separate "Union-Snyder Drug and Alcohol" office or "Snyder County Drug and Alcohol Commission."** The Single County Authority is CMSU (Columbia, Montour, Snyder and Union), HQ in Danville (Montour County — out of scope as a listing), with a Snyder annex in Middleburg.
4. **Pennsylvania Counseling Services and Crossroads Counseling have no Snyder County site in the evidence found** — nearest are Harrisburg (Dauphin) and Williamsport (Lycoming) / State College (Centre). Both out of scope.
5. **Gaudenzia Middleburg's local number is 570-837-2208** (own site also shows central 717-238-4200 and helpline 833-976-4357 — those are not replacements for the local number).
6. **Walking Free Clubhouse, 8183 Route 522, Suite 10, Middleburg (570-975-5595), opened January 2025** and is the county's dedicated peer-recovery space (AA, NA, SMART Recovery, Celebrate Recovery, Nar-Anon, Families in Recovery). It was not in the retrievable PA 211 items checked — candidate addition, subject to the live-browser check.

## Method and limits

- **Method:** Public web only — search and page-text fetch; nobody contacted. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. Every address checked by exact street address, never by ZIP or town name alone.
- **Sources:** Retrievable PA 211 records (CMSU Middleburg; Firetree New Way of Life Snyder); CMSU, Gaudenzia, Firetree, AppleGate, White Deer Run and Marworth own sites; Union-Snyder Community Action Agency human-services directory (July 2025); Selinsgrove Area School District resources; PA DDAP / state resource guides; treatment aggregators as leads only.
- **Limits:** Partial pilot only (see honest-limit paragraph above). No treatment advice is given and no programme is claimed to work. No bed/placement availability is reported — intake process only. No organisation is called closed/defunct from a failed page load or failed search. Individual AA/NA meetings are not listed as services — only meeting-list sources. Phone numbers conflict across sources for several rows and are flagged, not silently resolved.
