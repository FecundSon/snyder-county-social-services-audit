# Snyder County, PA — Utilities Assistance: Pilot List (as found, BEFORE checking)

**County:** Snyder County, PA (county seat Middleburg; largest town Selinsgrove; other towns Shamokin Dam, Freeburg, Port Trevorton, McClure, Beaver Springs, Richfield, Mount Pleasant Mills, Kratzerville, Paxtonville, Beavertown, Hummels Wharf).
**Category:** Utilities assistance (LIHEAP, shut-off help, fuel funds, weatherization).
**Date compiled:** 2026-11-15.
**Directory:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Status:** NOT SENT YET. Nothing in this file has been sent to PA 211 or any organisation.

> **Honest limit:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Snyder County check is outstanding before any send.

## How this list was built

Pilot rows were assembled from retrievable PA 211 search pages (Utility Assistance, Electric/Gas Service Payment Assistance, "I need help paying my utility bills"), the Union-Snyder Community Action Agency Human Services Directory (July 2025), the Union & Snyder County Reentry Resource Directory (2nd ed., June 2025), the Union-Snyder Agency on Aging older-adult resource guide (May 2025), and the utilities' and DHS's own programme pages. Each row records the claim **as found** — directory/secondary claim first — before any own-site check. Own-site findings are in `audit-report-2026-11-15.md`, not here.

## Territory note (checked 2026-11-15)

- **PPL Electric** serves all 21 municipalities in Snyder County (electric).
- **UGI Penn Natural Gas** serves only part of the county: Selinsgrove and Shamokin Dam boroughs, and Middlecreek, Monroe and Penn townships (UGI Gas tariff, Pa. P.U.C. No. 7, Supplement No. 62, effective March 29, 2026). Wikipedia adds Jackson Township; the tariff does not list it. UGI programmes are therefore NOT countywide in Snyder County.
- Lewisburg, Milton, Mifflinburg and Lewistown are in other counties (Union, Northumberland, Mifflin) and are OUT OF SCOPE as locations; organisations based there appear below only where they are recorded as serving Snyder County residents.

## Key season facts used as the checking baseline (to verify, not assume)

- LIHEAP 2026–27 season: opened Mon Nov 2, 2026, runs through Apr 23, 2027. Cash grants $200–$1,000; crisis grants $25 minimum, $1,000 season maximum. Helpline 1-866-857-7095. (PA DHS FY 2027 Final State Plan; pa.gov LIHEAP page.)
- Dollar Energy Fund Hardship Program 2026–27: opened Oct 1, 2026, runs through Sep 30, 2027, first-come first-served while funds last. (Dollar Energy Fund own site.)
