## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
1. **There is no AAA office in Middleburg.** The Union-Snyder Agency
   on Aging, Inc. — the AAA for both Union and Snyder counties — has
   its only published office at **116 N. 2nd St, Lewisburg, PA 17837**
   (Union County), **570-524-2100**, per its own site's footer on
   every page. Snyder County residents are given a second number,
   **570-374-5558**. Any listing showing a Snyder County AAA office
   in Middleburg is wrong; publish Lewisburg plus the Snyder line.
2. **Penns Creek Adult Resource Center — town/ZIP and hours both
   conflict.** The agency's own calendar page gives **3551 Richard
   Road, Penns Creek, PA 17862**, phone **570-837-6200**, hours
   **8AM–2PM, Mondays, Wednesdays through Fridays**. But the agency's
   own Older Adult Resources guide (May 30, 2025) gives the same
   centre as Mon, Wed–Fri **9:00am–2:00pm**, and several directories
   list it as Middleburg, PA 17842. Publish Penns Creek, PA 17862
   (own site), and flag the 8am vs 9am start for a further check.
   Note: the centre is closed every Tuesday.
3. **Beaver Springs Senior Center hours corrected.** Own calendar
   page: **55 Elm St, Beaver Springs, PA 17812, 570-658-7333,
   9AM–2PM, Mondays through Thursdays**. Aggregators print Mon–Fri
   9:00am–6:00pm — wrong on both days and closing time. The agency's
   own November 2026 calendar confirms Mon–Thu 9AM–2PM.
4. **PA 211's PA MEDI record still says APPRISE.** PA 211's Medicare
   Information/Counseling record for Union-Snyder describes the
   programme as "Free health insurance counseling program (APPRISE)".
   APPRISE was renamed PA MEDI; the agency's own homepage uses PA
   MEDI throughout and offers appointments at 570-524-2100.
   Correct the record name to PA MEDI.
5. **Foster Grandparent direct number corrected.** PA 211 lists the
   Foster Grandparent Program at the AAA's general number
   570-524-2100. The agency's own volunteer page directs enquiries
   to Director Sarah Long at **570-374-9196** (slong@usaaa17.org).
   Publish 570-374-9196 for the programme; eligibility 55+, limited
   income, 4 hours a day, 3–5 days a week.
6. **Only three senior centres serve Snyder County — none in
   Middleburg, McClure or Freeburg.** The agency's own site lists
   Selinsgrove, Penns Creek and Beaver Springs only, confirmed by
   its November 2026 calendars. A secondary page claiming "senior
   centers in Selinsgrove, Middleburg, and Port Trevorton" is not
   supported by any agency source found. The Lewisburg Senior Center
   (570-523-6510) is in Union County and out of scope.
7. **Farmers Market vouchers — 2026 applications already closed.**
   PA 211: 5 x $5 vouchers, redeemable June 1–Nov 30, 2026, Union/
   Snyder residents 60+ (or turning 60 by Dec 31, 2026), applications
   due **Sept 24, 2026**. On the check date the application window
   is closed and vouchers expire Nov 30, 2026; do not publish this
   as an open programme without a fresh 2027 check. Funding cuts to
   the programme were reported by WNEP in June 2025.

## Other confirmed / clarified items
- **Selinsgrove Senior Center confirmed:** 8 N. Water St,
  Selinsgrove, PA 17870, **570-374-4170, Tue–Thu 9AM–2PM** (own
  calendar page; manager Tara Bassler). PA 211's description
  (socialization, activities, exercise, hot nutritious meals,
  Bingo, pool, shuffleboard, chair aerobics, cards) matches.
- **PA MEDI confirmed:** free Medicare counselling via USAAA;
  appointments at **570-524-2100**; coordinator Janelle Werner,
  **570-837-1698** (jwerner@usaaa17.org). Open enrolment runs to
  Dec 7. PA MEDI counsellors can also check eligibility for
  PACE/PACENET, Extra Help and Medicare savings programmes.
- **rabbittransit Union-Snyder Shared Ride confirmed:** phone
  **1-800-632-9063**; Union/Snyder office **713 Bridge St, Ste 11,
  Selinsgrove, PA 17870**, Mon/Wed/Thu/Fri 8:00am–4:30pm, Tue
  9:30am–4:00pm (own site). Senior Shared Ride 65+: medical/
  pharmacy/dialysis $1.50–$4.50 by zone; senior-centre trips $0.25
  any zone; other trips $3.00–$7.50. No fixed-route service in
  Snyder County — shared ride only.
- **Health & Wellness confirmed:** "Live Your Best Life" (chronic
  disease, 6 weeks x 2.5 hrs), "Live Your Best Life with Diabetes",
  Healthy Steps fall prevention (50+, esp. 60+), A Matter of Balance
  — all free, at senior centres or community sites; info via
  570-524-2100 / 570-374-5558, Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:00pm. A Healthy
  Steps workshop was scheduled Nov 19–20, 2026 at Beaver Springs
  Senior Center (registration via 570-524-2100). Participants may
  live in any county for these programmes.
- **Protective Services clarified:** programme confirmed on the
  agency's own services page; PA 211 gives the local route as
  **570-524-2100** (investigates referrals within 72 hours,
  anonymous reports accepted). The statewide 24/7 elder abuse
  helpline **1-800-490-8505** is verified in the PA Department of

  agency's own site.
- **Ombudsman clarified:** programme confirmed on the agency's own
  services page; Supervisor Amy Foster, afoster@usaaa17.org,
  **570-524-2100**. No separate ombudsman intake number appears on
  the agency's own site — the general AAA number is the published
  route. (State Long-Term Care Ombudsman office 717-783-8975 is a
  secondary source only.)
- **Caregiver programmes clarified:** Family Caregiver Support
  Program exists (information, access help, individual counselling,
  support-group organisation, training, respite, supplemental
  services on a limited basis; assessment by USAAA required),
  570-524-2100 — but no specific support group, schedule or RSVP

  Support exists (reimbursement for child care, respite, equipment,
  counselling, tutoring, school expenses; grandparents/older
  relatives excluding parents), 570-524-2100; the 55+ caregiver
  threshold is PA 211 wording, not confirmed on the agency's own
  site.
- **Congregate meals clarified:** hot nutritious meals at the three
  senior centres for 60+ and spouses are confirmed in substance

- **Home-delivered meals (USAAA) clarified:** the programme exists
  (own services page; resource guide), intake via the AAA, but no
  eligibility detail, delivery days or intake procedure appears on
  the agency's own site. Local press (Daily Item, Oct 14, 2026)

- **OPTIONS / in-home services clarified:** own services page lists

  moves. No eligibility, cost-share, intake route or waiting-list

- **Benefits help clarified:** no standalone benefits-help listing

  which cover PACE/PACENET eligibility. Free Property Tax/Rent

- **PA LINK clarified:** statewide helpline **1-800-753-8827**

  publish them.
