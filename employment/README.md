- **No walk-in PA CareerLink in Snyder County.** Snyder's CareerLink
  is PA CareerLink Northumberland / Snyder / Union Counties, 225 Market
  St, Sunbury, PA 17801, 570-988-7300 (Sunbury is Northumberland
  County). Do not list a Snyder walk-in CareerLink address.
- **Old Selinsgrove CareerLink is not current.** The 2009 list's
  Selinsgrove CareerLink (713 Bridge St, Suite 11, 570-374-5751) does
  not appear on Advance Central PA's current six-office list; 713
  Bridge St is now the Union-Snyder CAA and a Link stop. Same for the
  old Lewisburg CareerLink — no Lewisburg office on the current list.
- **In Snyder County, CareerLink arrives on The Link mobile unit.**
  List its monthly schedule (e.g. Nov 2026: Snyder County Courthouse,
  Middleburg 11/3; Union-Snyder CAA, Selinsgrove 11/19; both
  10am–2pm), not a fixed office or fixed weekly hours. Coordinator
  Robert A. Eddy, 570-220-0003.
- **CSIU LPN Career Center has moved.** PA 211 still lists 1339 St
  Mary St, Suite 2, Lewisburg; the centre's own site says it is "newly
  relocated in Milton, Pa.", 15 Lawton Ln, Milton, 570-768-4960
  (Milton is Northumberland County). Confirm live before sending.
- **CSO is not Snyder's Community Action Agency.** Central
  Susquehanna Opportunities (Shamokin, Northumberland County) serves
  Columbia, Montour and Northumberland Counties and runs EARN at the
  Sunbury CareerLink. Snyder's CAA is Union-Snyder CAA, 713 Bridge St,
  Suite 10, Selinsgrove, 570-374-0181 — do not merge the two.
- **Union-Snyder CAA Work Ready is referral-based (TANF via the
  County Assistance Office), not general walk-in job help.**
- **OVR for Snyder is Williamsport (general) and Altoona (blindness/
  visual services), not Harrisburg.** Williamsport: Grit Building,
  Suite 102, 208 W 3rd St, 570-327-3600. Altoona BBVS: 1130 12th Ave,
  Suite 300, 814-946-7330.
- **SUN Tech is in Union County, not Snyder County.** 815 E Market St,
  New Berlin, 570-966-1031 — Snyder students/adults are served, but
  the site must never be presented as in Snyder County.

## Method and limits
Pilot list built from retrievable statewide PA 211 entries, Advance
Central PA's own site (locations page fetched 2026-11-15), the pa.gov
OVR office directory, the Union-Snyder CAA Human Services directory
(July 2025), and the Union & Snyder County Reentry Coalition guide
(June 2025); each item checked against the organisation's own
website/page. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party
aggregator. County for every address was verified by exact geocoding,
never by ZIP or town name alone. No organisation was contacted.
Three page-text fetches in this run hit a 429 rate limit and were not
retried; no finding depends on a retried fetch. The LPN relocation and
old-CareerLink findings rest on own-site pages as returned in search
results plus the fetched Advance Central PA locations page, and are
flagged for live confirmation before any send.

PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull
a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on
retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own
sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Snyder
County check is outstanding before any send.
