# Snyder County, PA — Transportation audit

Date checked: 2026-11-15. Status: NOT SENT YET — staged only. Nothing in this report has been submitted to PA 211 or to any organisation.
County: Snyder County, PA (county seat Middleburg; largest town Selinsgrove; other towns Shamokin Dam, Freeburg, Port Trevorton, McClure, Beaver Springs, Richfield, Mount Pleasant Mills, Kratzerville, Paxtonville, Beavertown, Hummels Wharf).
Category: Transportation.
Directory: statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
Method: for each listing, the directory / secondary claim was recorded first (pilot-list.md) and checked against the organisation's OWN website where possible. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. Public web only (browser.search + browser.open page-text fetch). No live browser tasks, no sign-ins, no purchases, no contact with any organisation. All fares, schedules and booking rules are date-stamped 2026-11-15 and are not permanent facts.
Auditor note: the auditor is NOT a local resident of Snyder County. Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).

> **Honest limit.** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Snyder County check is outstanding before any send.

## Headline findings

- **25 listings audited: 6 CONFIRMED, 3 CORRECTED, 10 CLARIFIED, 3 UNVERIFIED, 3 OUT OF SCOPE. In scope: 22.**
- **Shared-ride operator — rabbittransit (Susquehanna Regional Transportation Authority, SRTA).** The public/shared-ride transit provider serving Snyder County is rabbittransit, not a Snyder County–only operator. rabbittransit merged with the Union/Snyder Transportation Alliance (USTA) on July 1, 2016, and its own site and the SEDA-COG Coordinated Plan (Oct 2024) both state it provides shared-ride service to Snyder County residents (Senior Shared Ride, PwD, AAA, MATP, MH/IDD, ADA, veterans, general public). Local office: 713 Bridge St., Ste. #11, Selinsgrove, PA 17870; customer service / scheduling 1-800-632-9063.
- **MATP administrator — rabbittransit (SRTA), direct contractor to PA DHS.** For Snyder County, MATP is administered by rabbittransit under a direct contract with the Pennsylvania Department of Human Services (DHS MATP staff list groups Snyder under "Rabbit Transit"; NEMT Guide summary of the DHS MATP Broker Study, June 25, 2026, lists Snyder among the counties under rabbittransit's direct agreement). Scheduling / customer service: **1-800-632-9063** (weekdays 7:00 AM–7:00 PM, Saturday 7:45 AM–3:00 PM). MATP fare is $0.00 to the rider on rabbittransit's Union-Snyder fare chart (2026-11-15). Do NOT use STEP, Inc. for Snyder MATP — STEP administers MATP only in Lycoming and Clinton counties — and do NOT use the Indiana-County IndiGO number (724) 801-8857 that appears on a PA 211 MATP record.
- **Correction 1 — the old operator name is stale: Union-Snyder Transportation Alliance (USTA) → rabbittransit.** Any listing still filed only under "Union-Snyder Transportation Alliance" / "USTA" (Wikitravel Selinsgrove; 2011 SEDA-COG report; older guides) describes the pre-2016 operator. The operator since the July 1, 2016 merger is rabbittransit (SRTA). PA 211 should cross-reference USTA to rabbittransit, not list it as a separate current operator.
- **Correction 2 — Union-Snyder shared-ride fares have risen; older figures are stale.** rabbittransit's live Union-Snyder fare chart, fetched 2026-11-15 (rabbittransit.org/services/shared-ride-paratransit/fares/union-snyder-county/), shows: Senior Shared Ride 65+ medical/pharmacy/dialysis $1.50 / $2.50 / $3.00 / $4.50 (Zones 1–4); senior centre $0.25 all zones; senior other purposes $3.00 / $4.50 / $6.00 / $7.50; Persons with Disabilities $3.00 / $4.50 / $6.00 / $7.50; MATP $0.00; General Public (full fare) $20.00 / $30.00 / $40.00 / $50.00. A cached fare-adjustment chart still circulating shows the pre-rise figures (senior other purposes $2.25 / $3.75 / $4.50 / $6.75; general public $15 / $25 / $30 / $45). Those older figures must not be published as current.
- **Correction 3 — Stop Hopper fare: $2.50 cash / $2.25 app, not $2.00.** rabbittransit's own Stop Hopper page, fetched 2026-11-15, states $2.50 cash per one-way trip, $2.25 when paid in advance via the rabbitPAY / Stop Hopper app, free for seniors 65+ with a registered Free Fare ID card; Selinsgrove/Sunbury zone Mon–Fri 6:30 AM–6:30 PM. The Union-Snyder Agency on Aging transportation page still states "$2.00 per ride" — that figure is stale and conflicts with the operator's own site. Own site wins; the AAA page should be updated.
- **Clarification (systemic) — MATP is medical-only; shared ride is not a walk-on bus.** MATP covers transport to Medical Assistance–covered medical services only — not grocery, social or other non-medical trips — and is not general public transport. Conversely, rabbittransit shared ride (including Senior Shared Ride and PwD) is door-to-door, advance-reservation, application-based transport, not a fixed-route bus a rider can board without registering. Any directory entry blurring these two is wrong. Shared-ride reservations must be made by 1:30 PM the business day before the trip (by 1:30 PM Friday for Sat/Sun/Mon trips); bookings up to two weeks ahead; call 1-800-632-9063 — voicemail requests are not accepted.
- **Clarification (systemic) — there is no fixed-route bus in Snyder County in this pilot.** No fixed-route service operating only inside Snyder County was evidenced. The county's public options are (a) rabbittransit demand-response shared ride, (b) rabbittransit Stop Hopper microtransit in the Selinsgrove/Sunbury zone only, and (c) one intercity flag stop (Fullington Trailways / Greyhound, below). Do not list Snyder County as having a local fixed-route bus.
- **Clarification — Greyhound at Selinsgrove is a flag stop operated by Fullington Trailways, not a Greyhound station.** Greyhound lists "Selinsgrove Flag Stop, 105 S Market St, Selinsgrove, PA 17870, (800) 231-2222". The buses that actually serve it are Fullington Trailways FAB 26 (departs 7:10 AM toward Harrisburg) and FAB 29 (departs 4:10 PM toward Williamsport), both flag stops. Trailways booking pages label the stop "Selinsgrove (Amtrak)" — there is no Amtrak train service in Selinsgrove; that label is misleading and should not be repeated as a rail connection.
- **Clarification — the Lewisburg–Milton Stop Hopper zone is not a Snyder County service.** rabbittransit lists a second zone under "Columbia, Montour, Snyder & Union County" covering Berwick–Bloomsburg, Danville–Bloomsburg and Lewisburg–Milton. Lewisburg is Union County and Milton is Northumberland County. Snyder County residents are served by the separate Selinsgrove/Sunbury zone; the Lewisburg–Milton zone must not be presented as countywide Snyder coverage.
- **Clarification — PA 211 lists the same rabbittransit service under many out-of-county office addresses.** PA 211 records for rabbittransit Paratransit Shared Ride appear at York (415 N. Zarfoss Dr), Harrisburg (901 N. Cameron St), Chambersburg (191 Franklin Farm Ln), Bloomsburg (702 Sawmill Rd, Ste. 201) and Elysburg (61 Tyler Ave) as well as Selinsgrove. Those are offices of one regional service, not separate Snyder County services. Only the Selinsgrove office (713 Bridge St., Ste. #11) is in Snyder County.
- **No organisation was called closed or defunct.** Failed or unretrieved page loads are marked UNVERIFIED, never treated as closure.

## What this report does NOT claim

- This is NOT a complete list of Snyder County transportation services. PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch returned snippets, not complete per-record detail; the pilot is anchored on PA 211 items actually retrieved plus clearly-labelled secondary lists.
- No fare, schedule or route in this report is permanent. All are date-stamped 2026-11-15; rabbittransit fares had already risen from the figures in the cached adjustment chart, Fullington states its times are approximate and not guaranteed, and Stop Hopper's fare on the AAA page already conflicts with the operator's own site — every fare must be re-checked before any send or publication.
- Where fare figures conflicted across sources, the hierarchy was applied (own site beats directory beats aggregator) and the conflict is flagged in the entry; for STEP's booking lead time (noon vs 2:00 PM in two STEP sources) neither figure is published as a Snyder fact.
- No organisation was called closed or defunct. A failed page load or an unretrieved detail is marked UNVERIFIED, never treated as closure. In particular, R&D Ride Service, Smoley Van Service and Uber/Lyft availability are UNVERIFIED, not "closed" and not "operating".
- No organisation was contacted, and nothing was sent to PA 211 or anyone else. This audit is staged only.
- The auditor is NOT a local resident of Snyder County, and no residency is claimed or implied.
- Secondary sources (Wikipedia, SEDA-COG plans, CAA directory, Wikitravel, findhelp, Uber, Facebook, aggregators) are labelled as secondary wherever used; they do not outrank an organisation's own site.
- County scope was judged by exact address / stated service area, never by ZIP or town name alone. Lewisburg, Milton, Mifflinburg and Lewistown are in other counties; services based there (Golden Rule Love Inc., HandUP, Union-Snyder AAA, STEP, Fullington Lewisburg stops) are in scope only where their stated service area includes Snyder County residents, and that is stated per entry.
