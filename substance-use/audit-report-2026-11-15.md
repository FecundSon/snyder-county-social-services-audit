## Headline findings (only own-site-evidenced, material corrections)

1. **Conewago Snyder residential treatment is for men aged 18+ only (28 beds), not co-ed — aggregators saying "co-ed" and "42-bed" are wrong for the residential programme.** Firetree's own site (firetree.com/conewago-snyder) says "Serves men 18 years and older" and "28 bed male inpatient capacity". The detox unit on the same campus has accepted women since 1 Oct 2023 (Firetree news), which is the likely source of the co-ed confusion — residential and detox must not be conflated.
2. **PA 211's CMSU listing implies CMSU itself directly provides inpatient detoxification, inpatient rehabilitation and a halfway house; CMSU's own site says its case managers assess and refer, and CMSU contracts with treatment providers for a complete continuum of care.** The Middleburg address, phone, hours and intake route are confirmed; the service list needs qualifying, not deleting.
3. **There is no separate "Union-Snyder Drug and Alcohol" office or "Snyder County Drug and Alcohol Commission" as a distinct organisation in the evidence found.** The Single County Authority for Snyder County is CMSU (Columbia, Montour, Snyder and Union), headquartered at 507 East Market Street, Danville (Montour County), with a Snyder annex at 858 Sale Barn Road, Middleburg. The Danville HQ is out of scope as a physical listing even though it serves Snyder County.
4. **Pennsylvania Counseling Services and Crossroads Counseling have no Snyder County site in the evidence found.** The nearest Pennsylvania Counseling Services site in the pilot sources is Harrisburg (Dauphin County); Crossroads Counseling's nearest sites are Williamsport (Lycoming County) and State College (Centre County). Both are out of scope as listings.
5. **Gaudenzia Middleburg's local number is 570-837-2208; Gaudenzia's own site also publishes a central number (717) 238-4200 and a Treatment and Referral HelpLine 833-976-HELP (4357).** The local number is confirmed by CARF and by Gaudenzia's own COVID plan; the central/helpline numbers are not a correction to the local number, but PA 211 should not replace the local number with the central one.
6. **Walking Free Clubhouse, 8183 Route 522, Suite 10, Middleburg (phone 570-975-5595), opened January 2025 and is the county's dedicated peer-recovery space** — hosting AA, NA, SMART Recovery, Celebrate Recovery, Nar-Anon and Families in Recovery. It does not appear in the retrievable PA 211 items checked; it is a candidate addition, subject to the live-browser PA 211 check.

## What this report does NOT claim

- **Not a complete county directory.** This is a partial pilot: PA 211's JavaScript search pages cannot be pulled completely by text fetch, so the pilot is anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites. Other Snyder County substance-use services may exist and are not in this report.
- **No treatment advice, and no claim that any programme works.** This report records contact details, stated services and intake routes only. It does not recommend any provider, level of care or medication, and it does not assess quality or outcomes.
- **No bed or placement availability.** Availability changes constantly. Only the intake process (who to call, referral requirements) is recorded, never "beds available".
- **No organisation is called closed or defunct.** Rows marked UNVERIFIED mean the evidence found was insufficient or conflicting — a failed page load or a failed search is not evidence of closure.
- **No private treatment details are published.** No individual, client or patient information appears in this report.
- **Individual AA/NA meetings are not listed as services.** Only meeting-list sources are recorded, with times/venues expressly unverified.
- **Hours are only stated where a source stated them.** Absence of hours in an entry means the source did not state them, not that the service has no hours.
- **Phone numbers are as published by the cited source on 2026-11-15.** Numbers conflict across sources for several rows (notably AA, Al-Anon, Gaudenzia Sunbury, White Deer Run, AppleGate, Pyramid Lewisburg); conflicting numbers are flagged in the entries, not silently resolved.
- **County boundaries are by street address, not ZIP or town name.** Several nearby towns share names/ZIPs across county lines; every out-of-scope verdict rests on the street address's county (Danville = Montour; Sunbury/Milton = Northumberland; Lewisburg/Allenwood = Union; Williamsport = Lycoming; Waverly = Lackawanna; Harrisburg = Dauphin; Lewistown = Mifflin).
