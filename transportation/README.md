## Headline corrections

1. **Shared-ride operator is rabbittransit (Susquehanna Regional Transportation Authority, SRTA) — not USTA.** The Union-Snyder Transportation Alliance merged into rabbittransit on July 1, 2016. Any listing still filed only under "Union-Snyder Transportation Alliance" / "USTA" is stale and should be cross-referenced to rabbittransit. Local office: 713 Bridge St., Ste. #11, Selinsgrove, PA 17870; scheduling 1-800-632-9063.
2. **MATP administrator for Snyder County is rabbittransit (SRTA), direct contractor to PA DHS — scheduling 1-800-632-9063, fare $0.00 to the rider.** Do not use STEP, Inc. (Lycoming/Clinton MATP only) or the Indiana-County IndiGO number (724) 801-8857 for Snyder MATP.
3. **Union-Snyder shared-ride fares have risen.** Live rabbittransit chart, 2026-11-15: Senior 65+ other purposes $3.00 / $4.50 / $6.00 / $7.50 (Zones 1–4); senior medical/pharmacy/dialysis $1.50 / $2.50 / $3.00 / $4.50; senior centre $0.25; PwD $3.00 / $4.50 / $6.00 / $7.50; general public $20 / $30 / $40 / $50. Older figures ($2.25 / $3.75 / $4.50 / $6.75 senior; $15 / $25 / $30 / $45 general public) are stale.
4. **Stop Hopper fare is $2.50 cash / $2.25 app, not $2.00.** rabbittransit's own site (2026-11-15) vs the Union-Snyder Agency on Aging page, which still states $2.00. Selinsgrove/Sunbury zone, Mon–Fri 6:30 AM–6:30 PM; free for seniors 65+ with a Free Fare ID card.
5. **Greyhound at Selinsgrove is a Fullington-operated flag stop, not a station — and "Selinsgrove (Amtrak)" is a misleading label.** 105 S. Market St, Selinsgrove; Fullington FAB 26 departs 7:10 AM to Harrisburg, FAB 29 departs 4:10 PM to Williamsport. No Amtrak train serves Selinsgrove.
6. **MATP is medical-only; shared ride is application + advance reservation, not a walk-on bus.** Shared-ride reservations by 1:30 PM the business day before (Friday for Sat/Sun/Mon); voicemail requests not accepted. There is no fixed-route bus in Snyder County in this pilot — only shared ride, Stop Hopper microtransit (Selinsgrove/Sunbury zone), and the one intercity flag stop.

## Method and limits

- Pilot list built first from retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists (Union-Snyder CAA Directory, July 2025; SEDA-COG Coordinated Plan, Oct 2024; PA DHS MATP materials), then each row checked against the organisation's own website where possible. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. Public web only; nobody contacted.
- County scope by exact address / stated service area, never by ZIP or town name alone. Lewisburg, Milton, Mifflinburg and Lewistown are in other counties; services based there are in scope only where they serve Snyder County residents.
- All fares, schedules and booking rules are date-stamped 2026-11-15. Where figures conflicted, the hierarchy was applied and the conflict flagged; for STEP's booking lead time (noon vs 2:00 PM in two STEP sources) neither figure is published as a Snyder fact.
- No organisation was called closed or defunct; unretrieved details are UNVERIFIED.
