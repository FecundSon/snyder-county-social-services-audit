## Headline corrections
1. **No AAA office in Middleburg.** The Union-Snyder Agency on Aging
   (the AAA for Union and Snyder counties) is based at 116 N. 2nd St,
   Lewisburg, PA 17837 (Union County), 570-524-2100; Snyder residents
   use 570-374-5558.
2. **Penns Creek Adult Resource Center — town/ZIP conflict.** Own site
   says 3551 Richard Rd, Penns Creek, PA 17862; directories say
   Middleburg, PA 17842. Own site also conflicts on hours start
   (8am on the centre page vs 9am in the agency's guide).
3. **Beaver Springs Senior Center hours corrected.** Own site: Mon–Thu
   9AM–2PM, 55 Elm St, Beaver Springs, 570-658-7333. Aggregators
   printing Mon–Fri 9am–6pm are wrong.
4. **PA 211's PA MEDI record still says APPRISE.** APPRISE was renamed
   PA MEDI; correct the record name.
5. **Foster Grandparent direct number corrected.** Own site: Director
   Sarah Long, 570-374-9196 — not PA 211's general 570-524-2100.
6. **Only three senior centres serve Snyder County** (Selinsgrove,
   Penns Creek, Beaver Springs). No centre in Middleburg, McClure or
   Freeburg was found; the Lewisburg centre is in Union County.
7. **Farmers Market vouchers — 2026 applications closed Sept 24,
   2026;** vouchers expire Nov 30, 2026. Do not publish as open.

## Method and limits
Pilot list built from retrievable PA 211 records, the Union-Snyder
Agency on Aging's own site (usaaa17.org), rabbittransit's own site,
PA Department of Aging publications, and secondary directories
(Union-Snyder Community Action Agency Human Services directory,
July 2025). Every item was then checked against the organisation's
own website/page via public web search and page-text fetch. Own site
beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. No
organisation was contacted. A failed or missing page is UNVERIFIED,
never closure — no organisation is called closed or defunct. Addresses
were verified by exact address, not ZIP or town name alone; Lewisburg,
Milton, Mifflinburg and Lewistown are in other counties and out of
scope. Contact information and listing accuracy only — no health,
benefits or legal advice.
