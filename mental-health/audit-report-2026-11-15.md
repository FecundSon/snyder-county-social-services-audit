# Snyder County, PA — Mental Health / Crisis Services: Audit Report

**County:** Snyder County, PA (county seat Middleburg; largest town Selinsgrove; other towns Shamokin Dam, Freeburg, Port Trevorton, McClure, Beaver Springs, Richfield, Mount Pleasant Mills, Kratzerville, Paxtonville, Beavertown, Hummels Wharf)
**Category:** Mental health / crisis services
**Date checked:** 2026-11-15
**Directory:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org)
**Status:** NOT SENT YET — nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211 or to any organisation. No organisation was contacted.
**Auditor:** not a local resident of Snyder County. Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).

> **HONEST LIMIT:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Snyder County check is outstanding before any send.

## Method

Public web only — search and page-text fetch; nobody contacted. Pilot list (28 rows) built from retrievable PA 211 record pages, the Union–Snyder Community Action Agency *Human Services* directory (July 2025), the Susquehanna Kids mental-health guide, PA DHS / PA Department of Education county lists, and organisations' own sites. Each listing was then checked against the organisation's own website or own page. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. Every street address was verified by exact geocoding (county returned by the geocoder is recorded per row); towns in other counties — Lewisburg, Milton, Mifflinburg, Lewistown, Danville, Sunbury, Bloomsburg, Wilkes-Barre — were treated as out of scope as *locations*, even where the service serves Snyder County residents.

**Checking limitation on 2026-11-15:** after four page fetches, the page-text service returned rate-limit errors (429) for further `browser.open` calls, so several own sites (Courtyard Counseling, Step By Step Counseling, Mid River Counseling, Hempfield Behavioral Services, True North, Geisinger Behavioral Health Centers) could not be opened directly. For those rows the verdict is UNVERIFIED (private practices) or is based on search-result snippets from the organisation's own domain, and that is stated per row. Nothing was inferred from a failed load, and no organisation was called closed or defunct on that basis.

**Verdicts:** CONFIRMED (directory claim matches own site) / CORRECTED (own-site evidence contradicts the directory claim on a material point) / CLARIFIED (claim is broadly right but misleading, incomplete, or needs a location/scope qualifier) / UNVERIFIED (no own-site evidence obtained) / OUT OF SCOPE (address outside Snyder County with no Snyder County site).

Note: entry numbers in this report follow the report's own thematic order and differ from the pilot list's numbering (which follows source groups); entries are matched by organisation name, not by number.

3. **Gaudenzia Middleburg Outpatient — the local number is 570-837-2208, not the numbers shown on aggregator / corporate pages.** Recovery.com shows (877) 706-7641 (a national intake line); Gaudenzia's own place data shows 717-238-4200 (a Harrisburg-area number). ### County crisis line — flagged prominently

**The Snyder County crisis line is CMSU TAPline: 1-800-222-9016, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.** It is held by **CMSU Behavioral Health & Developmental Services** (Columbia, Montour, Snyder and Union Counties), confirmed on CMSU's own crisis page (cmsu.org/crisis-emergency, opened 2026-11-15), on the PA DHS county crisis-numbers list, and on the PA Department of Education county contacts page. It is a **separate number from 988** (the national Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, call or text 988) — the two must not be substituted for each other. Do **not** use (570) 275-4962 as a crisis number: that is CMSU's Danville administrative / referral line. No error in the crisis number itself was found in any source checked; the risk flagged is that PA 211's Middleburg listing displays both (570) 275-4962 and (570) 837-3102 on the same record, which could be read as a crisis contact — see row 6, CLARIFIED.
