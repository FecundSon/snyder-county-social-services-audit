## Notes on the pilot list

- **"Union-Snyder Drug and Alcohol" / "Snyder County Drug and Alcohol Commission":** no separate office with that name was found in the pilot sources. The Single County Authority (SCA) function for Snyder County is CMSU — Columbia, Montour, Snyder and Union — headquartered in Danville (Montour County) with a Snyder annex in Middleburg (#1/#2). Several aggregator pages use "Snyder County Drug and Alcohol Commission" loosely; that wording was not verified to a distinct organisation.
- **"Gaudenzia Selinsgrove, 1 Furnace Rd, Selinsgrove":** appears on one aggregator page (bestrehabcenternear.me, Hummels Wharf). No Gaudenzia own-site location page for a Selinsgrove site was found, and Gaudenzia's own locations list shows Middleburg and Sunbury in this area. It is NOT included as a pilot row; it is flagged for the pre-send re-check.
- **Duplicate-risk pairs in this list:** #1/#2 (same organisation, two PA 211 records, two addresses); #4/#5 (same Firetree campus, two programmes, one address); #3/#22/#23 (same provider, three towns — only #3 is in Snyder County); #9 is the crisis line of the same CMSU system as #1/#2/#8.
- Individual AA/NA meetings are NOT listed as services. Only the meeting-list sources are rows (#13/#14); meeting times/venues change and were not verified.
- No bed/placement availability is stated anywhere in this file. Availability changes constantly; only intake process is recorded.
- No treatment advice is given and no programme is claimed to work.
